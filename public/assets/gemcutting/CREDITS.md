# Gemcutting artwork

- Painted gemstones by [GOBI Studio](https://gobistudio.itch.io/free-shiny-gems-icon-pack), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Selected sprites were cropped and resized.
- [Magnifying Glass](https://game-icons.net/1x1/lorc/magnifying-glass.html), [Powder](https://game-icons.net/1x1/lorc/powder.html) and [Root Tip](https://game-icons.net/1x1/lorc/root-tip.html) by Lorc. [Pestle & Mortar](https://game-icons.net/1x1/skoll/pestle-mortar.html) by Skoll. [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The icons were recolored and rasterized.
- [Dark Wood](https://polyhaven.com/a/dark_wood) by Dario Barresi, Dimitrios Savva and Rico Cilliers. [Velour Velvet](https://polyhaven.com/a/velour_velvet) by colormass and Rico Cilliers. Both textures are [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The diffuse maps were resized for the game.
- The lapidary workbench illustration was generated for Path of Idle with the built-in image generation tool. The cutting wheel close-up is a crop of that illustration.

## Workbench generation prompt

> Create an original painted background for the Path of Idle dark-fantasy RPG Gemcutting station, file concept lapidary-bench-bg. Wide landscape, extra-wide banner composition: all important visual objects in the middle horizontal strip so the artwork can also be center-cropped to a 7:1 hero banner. Intimate medieval lamp-lit jeweler's workshop. A dark mahogany workbench with worn wood grain, a large antique brass horizontal faceting wheel with a circular lap plate on a low stand at center, and a brass jeweler's loupe next to it. Glowing natural amethyst, teal and emerald uncut crystals flank the wheel, on a dark aubergine velvet pad. Natural bronze patina and precision details, old-master oil-painted RPG environment with tactile material texture, strong silhouette clarity, rich aubergine shadows, restrained warm amber light and a few luminous mint reflections. Leave left and right edges shadowed so interface text will read there. Image only, no lettering, no logos, no UI, no border, no people or hands. It must feel like a usable medieval jewelcrafter's station.
