# Station artwork

Art for the illustrated Mining, Sawmilling, Greenhouse and Achievements screens.

- **Sawmilling banner** (`sawmill-bodom-falls.webp`): Erik Bodom, *Foss med sagbruk* (1878), via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Erik_Bodom_-_Foss_med_sagbruk.jpg). Public domain. Resized for the game.
- **Greenhouse banner** (`greenhouse-blechen-palmhouse.webp`): Carl Blechen, *The Interior of the Palm House on the Pfaueninsel near Potsdam*, [Art Institute of Chicago](https://www.artic.edu/artworks/144969). CC0 / public domain. A landscape band was cropped from the portrait painting and resized.
- **Textures** (`tex-planks.webp`, `tex-rusty-iron.webp`): [Brown Planks 09](https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_planks_09) and [Rusty Metal 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/rusty_metal_02) from Poly Haven, [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). The diffuse maps were resized.
- **Icons** from [game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net), [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), recoloured in the game:
  - by Lorc: [Circular Sawblade](https://game-icons.net/1x1/lorc/circular-sawblade.html) (`icon-buzzsaw.svg`)
  - by Delapouite: [Screw](https://game-icons.net/1x1/delapouite/screw.html) (`icon-rock-bolt.svg`), [Windpump](https://game-icons.net/1x1/delapouite/windpump.html) (`icon-pump.svg`), [Computer Fan](https://game-icons.net/1x1/delapouite/computer-fan.html) (`icon-vent-fan.svg`), [Greenhouse](https://game-icons.net/1x1/delapouite/greenhouse.html) (`icon-greenhouse.svg`), [Plant Seed](https://game-icons.net/1x1/delapouite/plant-seed.html) (`icon-seed.svg`), [Herbs Bundle](https://game-icons.net/1x1/delapouite/herbs-bundle.html) (`icon-herbs.svg`), [Trophy Cup](https://game-icons.net/1x1/delapouite/trophy-cup.html) (`icon-trophy.svg`)
