# Station artwork

Art for the illustrated Mining, Sawmilling, Smelting, Woodcrafting, Astrology, Greenhouse and Achievements screens.

- **Sawmilling banner** (`sawmill-hobbema-watermill.webp`): Meindert Hobbema, *The Watermill with the Great Red Roof* (c. 1665), [Art Institute of Chicago](https://www.artic.edu/artworks/869). CC0 / public domain. A landscape band was cropped from the painting, lightly brightened and resized.
- **Smelting banner** (`foundry-menzel-rolling-mill.webp`): Adolph Menzel, *Eisenwalzwerk (The Iron Rolling Mill / Modern Cyclopes)* (1872–1875), via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Adolph_Menzel_-_Eisenwalzwerk_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg). Public domain. A landscape band was cropped and resized for the game.
- **Woodcrafting banner** (`woodcraft-arrow-maker.webp`): Follower of Pieter Brueghel the Younger, *Pfeilschnitzer* (The Arrow-Maker, 16th century), via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pfeilschnitzer_(nach_Pieter_Brueghel_dJ).jpg) (source: Dorotheum). Public domain. A landscape band was cropped from the round painting and resized.
- **Astrology banner** (`observatory-spitzweg-stargazer.webp`): Carl Spitzweg, *The Astronomer (Stargazer)* (c. 1860), via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Carl_Spitzweg_-_The_Astronomer_(Stargazer)_-_c._1860.jpg). Public domain. A landscape band was cropped from the portrait painting and resized.
- **Skills banner, The War Table** (`war-table-kivshenko-fili.webp`): Aleksey Kivshenko, *Military Council at Fili* (1880), via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kutuzov_fili.jpg). Public domain. A landscape band was cropped, lightly brightened and resized.
- **Woodcutting banner** (`woodcutting-bough-cadzow.webp`): Samuel Bough, *Woodcutters, Cadzow Forest*, via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Samuel_Bough_-_Woodcutters,_Cadzow_Forest.jpg). Public domain. A landscape band was cropped and resized.
- **Thieving banner** (`thieving-caravaggio-cardsharps.webp`): Caravaggio, *The Cardsharps* (c. 1594), via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Caravaggio_(Michelangelo_Merisi)_-_The_Cardsharps_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg). Public domain. A landscape band was cropped, lightly brightened and resized.
- **Archaeology banner** (`archaeology-robert-ruins.webp`): Hubert Robert, *Imaginary View of the Grande Galerie in the Louvre in Ruins* (1796), via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hubert_Robert_-_Imaginary_View_of_the_Grande_Galerie_in_the_Louvre_in_Ruins_-_WGA19589.jpg). Public domain. A landscape band was cropped and resized.
- **Forging banner** (`forging-wright-iron-forge.webp`): Joseph Wright of Derby, *An Iron Forge* (1772), via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Joseph_Wright_-_An_Iron_Forge_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg). Public domain. A landscape band was cropped, brightened and resized.
- **Tailoring banner** (`tailoring-brekelenkam-workshop.webp`): Quiringh van Brekelenkam, *The Tailor's Workshop* (1661), Rijksmuseum, via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:De_kleermakerswerkplaats_Rijksmuseum_SK-C-112.jpeg). Public domain. A landscape band was cropped from the portrait painting and resized.
- **Alchemy banner** (`alchemy-teniers-alchemist.webp`): David Teniers the Younger, *The Alchemist*, Mauritshuis, via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:David_Teniers_de_Jonge_-_The_Alchemist_-_261_-_Mauritshuis.jpg). Public domain. A landscape band was cropped, lightly brightened and resized.
- **Greenhouse banner** (`greenhouse-blechen-palmhouse.webp`): Carl Blechen, *The Interior of the Palm House on the Pfaueninsel near Potsdam*, [Art Institute of Chicago](https://www.artic.edu/artworks/144969). CC0 / public domain. A landscape band was cropped from the portrait painting and resized.
- **Textures** (`tex-planks.webp`, `tex-rusty-iron.webp`): [Brown Planks 09](https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_planks_09) and [Rusty Metal 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/rusty_metal_02) from Poly Haven, [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). The diffuse maps were resized.
