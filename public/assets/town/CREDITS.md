# Town (The Exile's Bazaar) artwork

Stall banners: a landscape band was cropped from each painting, lightly brightened, resized and saved as WebP. The game darkens the edges in CSS.

- **Magic shop, Ilsa's curio stall** (`stall-curio.webp`): Jan Brueghel the Younger, *Allegory of Sight* (c. 1660), via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jan_Brueghel_the_Younger_-_Allegory_of_Sight,_c._1660.png). Public domain. The band shows the shelf of gilt statuettes and busts.
- **Weapon & Armour shop, Varro's armoury** (`stall-armoury.webp`): Jan Brueghel the Elder and Hendrick van Balen, *Allegory of Fire; Venus in the Forge of Vulcan*, via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jan_Brueghel_(I)_and_Hendrick_van_Balen_(I)_-_Allegory_of_Fire;_Venus_in_the_Forge_of_Vulcan.jpg). Public domain. The band shows the heap of armour and helmets.
- **Gem shop, the Gemwright's counter** (`stall-gemwright.webp`): Petrus Christus, *A Goldsmith in his Shop* (1449), The Metropolitan Museum of Art, via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_Goldsmith_in_his_Shop_MET_DT711.jpg). CC0 / public domain.
- **Sell tab, the Pawn Counter** (`stall-pawn.webp`): Quentin Massys, *The Moneylender and his Wife* (1514), via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Quinten_Massijs_(I)_-_The_Moneylender_and_his_Wife_-_WGA14281.jpg). Public domain.
- **Keeper portraits** (`keeper-*.png`): copies of 64px pixel avatars from Craftpix's Free Fantasy Character Avatar Icons (Pixel Art), [Craftpix file licence](https://craftpix.net/file-licenses/). `keeper-varro.png` is the existing trader portrait (`craftpix-con33`); Ilsa is `con12`, the Gemwright `con6` and the Pawn Counter `con1`.
