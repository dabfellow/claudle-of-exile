# Skilling overhaul implementation specification

Date: 2026-11-13; implementation updated 2026-11-14. Phase 00 design contract. **Phases 01–08 foundations, Mining, Woodcutting, Smithing, equipment checkpoint, Foraging/Greenhouse, Alchemy and Cooking verified locally/unreleased; Phases 09–18 remain pending.** Actual interfaces and evidence are recorded in §§13–20.

Read with [the reference](SKILLING_OVERHAUL.md), [phase plan and handoff](SKILLING_OVERHAUL_PLAN.md), and [tracking](MILESTONES.md). Existing behavior is documented in GAME and the source files below. This document specifies future behavior; it does not describe a release.

## 1. Authority, scope and notation

**Owner decisions:** useful campaign upgrades and an optional deep skilling path; known projects plus surprises; one selected ordinary task at a time; independent profession progression; equipment requirements remain; bonus-only mastery; current character/realm ownership and trading; existing exclusive reward sources remain. Ten unique concepts and their defining powers, drawbacks and broad routes were selected in reference questions 3–8. Fishing, Fletching, new professions, new gem rarities and incidental combat rebalance are excluded.

**Owner decision during Phase 00, topic 1 (2026-11-13):** “sum of all is the only divinable thing here”. The Sum of All Things is Divine-rerollable. All twelve other new uniques have fixed effects. This overrides the reference's proposed full-recipe-only reroll restriction and Phase 17 wording that would prohibit that Divine route. A second complete craft remains possible, but is not required to improve an existing amulet's roll. The three pinnacle names/effects remain assistant-authored designs under the owner's request for three single-effect, multi-mechanic chase items; do not retrospectively label those designs owner-authored.

**Assistant tuning/technical decisions:** all new IDs, numbers, levels, costs, pools, recipe structure, guarantees, encounter design, effect algorithms and timings below. Reasons are given with each system. These are implementable choices within the accepted direction, not additional owner decisions. Later agents may adjust overhaul numbers with measured evidence and a recorded reason; major changes to the experience use design-pass before dependent work. No major choice remains pending at this boundary.

Numbers in new recipe tables are **final awarded XP**, after the existing `SKILL_XP_SCALE = 0.3`. If authored in the current raw-data table, divide these numbers by 0.3 before its single scaling pass. Existing recipes retain their exact current interval, scaled XP, inputs, ordinary outputs and bonus probabilities unless an override below explicitly names them. The existing noncombat level-99 XP curve, character curve, mastery formula and skilling point budget do not change.

Recipe notation: `id | level | seconds / XP | input -> output`. Quantities omitted mean one. Inputs and outputs are literal stable IDs unless a finite expansion rule is defined. Material rewards go to `account.bank`; equipment, gems, flasks and Maps keep their existing storage type. `P` denotes a protected project input/output: never duplicated, conserved, elevated or refunded by ordinary yield bonuses. Costs and output previews must show this distinction. New action IDs below are literal; new project action IDs are `project_<projectId>`.

## 3. Shared access, ownership and completion contract (Phase 01)

### 3.1 Access and stations

Every ordinary action removes its campaign `area`/`gate`, including existing advanced flask and boss-key **manufacturing** recipes. Combat entry, character/attribute requirements, gem signatures, Classic restrictions and challenge realm isolation remain. All professions have a level-1 task. Milestones are monotonic character accomplishments separate from bonus-only task mastery.

New generic accomplishment IDs: `<skill>_work_<N>` records at least N **paid/completed foreground cycles** in that profession, including retained evidence inferred on migration below. It counts a batch by its number of single cycles, never its bonus output. Specific discoveries/job successes use IDs explicitly named below. No mastery level is ever an access requirement. Do not require a later profession to unlock an earlier supplier.

Required station changes are deliberately bounded:

| Station/level | Additional noncombat purchase route (cost replaces, not adds to old route) | Unlock |
|---|---|---|
| Forge 2 | gold 300, copper_bar 10, ash_plank 5 | Smithing 10, `smithing_work_20` |
| Forge 3 | gold 1200, iron_bar 20, oak_plank 10 | Smithing 22, `smithing_work_60` |
| Alchemy Table 2 | gold 300, sunpetal 40, ash_plank 5 | Alchemy 8, `alchemy_work_20` |
| Alchemy Table 3 | gold 1200, frostleaf 60, iron_bar 10 | Alchemy 18, `alchemy_work_60` |
| Rune Board 1 | already free/default; remove Act 3 use restriction | Rune-carving 1 |
| Greenhouse | no new station/cost; existing keystone and learned plant eligibility | existing tree allocation |

Keep old station purchase routes, already-owned levels, Forge 4–8 perks/costs, Alchemy 4–8 perks/costs, Rune Board 2–6, Bench, Watchtower and every other station unchanged. Required unique projects use at most Forge/Table 3 and Rune Board 1. No project requires Bench automation, a Rune Board upgrade, Map Device or combat trophies except explicit pinnacle/encounter components. Existing advanced station bonuses remain optional acceleration.

Gold has an unconditional independent source: `mark_peddler` at Thieving 1, 3 gold/3 seconds before bonuses. No crew or purchase is required. Bars/planks use ordinary starter gathering/refinement; iron/oak are level 15 with no zone gate. Rune flavours and other mandatory minor materials gain the deterministic routes in §5. The station graph is acyclic: starter actions -> bars/planks/gold -> station 2 -> level-15 ingredients -> station 3. Station 3 is not needed to make its own upgrade ingredients.

Smithing strength: `min(90, smithingLevel + existing FORGE_ILVL bonus + selected resin bonus, stationCap)` with station caps Forge 1 = 21, Forge 2 = 45, Forge 3+ = 90. Recipe level is at least base equip level; player may manufacture higher-requirement equipment but cannot equip it early. Preserve current Forge tier/affix chance bonuses. Reason: a level-90 independent smith can make ilvl-90 gear without buying combat-funded perks, while station progress still matters early.

### 3.2 Persistence and interfaces

Proposed character field `professionProgress` (schema 1): `{ work: Record<SkillId, number>, discoveries: Record<string, true>, counters: Record<string, number>, projects: Record<string, { completed: number }>, nextRun: number, activeProject?: ProjectRun }`. `ProjectRun` holds `runId`, project ID, recipe version, selected item UID if any, elapsed/required work, escrow costs, and committed reward status. Use a discriminated `action.kind = 'project'` occupying the same slot, integrated into state tick and offline activity detection. Keep ordinary `skill` actions unchanged unless they opt into typed reward metadata.

Conceptual APIs: `professionAccessReason`, `projectPreview`, `startProject`, `advanceProject`, `cancelProject`, `completeProject`, `recordDiscovery`, `awardProfessionProgress`. Names are proposed interfaces for Phase 01; publish actual names in its handoff. Project definitions declare owner skill, level, accomplishments, station, inputs, duration, XP, finite reward category and repeatability. Do not build unfinished later-phase recipes just to expose an interface.

Ownership: XP, mastery, unlocks, work counters, project runs, seed discoveries and crew/faction progress remain character-owned. Materials, equipment supply, loose gems/flasks, Maps and stations remain realm-owned through existing account paths. A material can be shared/traded under existing rules without transferring the recipient's profession accomplishment. No tradable discovery/claim tokens. Project materials in the bank are ordinary transferable material records; the protected category controls earning, not trade policy. SSF/challenge restrictions continue using existing helpers; Classic still cannot start life-skill work. New projects must not evade that blocker.

### 3.3 Safe completion and reward categories

| Category | Ordinary bonuses/manual double | Persistence and progress |
|---|---|---|
| ordinary material, currency, cheap base, regular gem/flask/rune/meal/map | preserve existing applicable tree/mastery/station/manual rules | ordinary material output and XP; work/practice credited once per base cycle |
| deterministic material progress, e.g. `heartwood_fragment` | exactly listed quantity; optional independent extra-find roll only where specified | counter once per nominal completed cycle; never “every bonus output” |
| discovery/accomplishment | never doubled | monotonic set membership; grant once across reload |
| project completion/unique/unique chart/signature/tool | never doubled, refunded, elevated or conserved | exactly one completion per run ID, fixed full cost |

For ordinary timed production, recheck full nominal affordability and all destination capacity before payment. Compute the actual conserved cost once, reserve capacity for the advertised base output and actual rolled extra output, then pay/grant in one synchronous mutation. Optional extra equipment without space is suppressed with an explicit summary message; never lose the base promised result or overfill inventory. Missing nominal inputs stops even if a random conservation roll could make that cycle free. Preserve existing cheap-base intentional salvage behavior; guaranteed-purpose/project gear is excluded from salvage-all/miss/filter handling.

Projects escrow the full displayed cost on start, including one chosen base UID for inscriptions. No partial automatic funding from future supplies. Ordinary yield/cost modifiers, holiday crafting discounts, masteries, mini-games and batch multipliers do not alter protected costs/rewards. Lock escrowed UID against crafting, equipping, trading and reuse. Cancellation returns exactly escrow (same original object/UID), discards that run's partial work, grants no XP/reward; inability to return gear uses a single pending return until space exists. Material refunds go to realm bank. Deleting a character follows current deletion semantics, not a newly invented realm claim.

At work completion validate delivery, prepare output, then commit consumption, completion record, UID allocation and output together. Full bag pauses at `ready: storage full` with escrow intact and no completed claim; move no finished equipment to lossy ground/haul storage. Material-only protected output can deliver immediately. A blocked completed project retains work; restarting/loading cannot charge again or create another result. Repeatable projects require a fresh run ID and new payment; default is stop after one. An explicitly selected finite repeat count may begin the same project only, with a fresh affordability/capacity check; never select a supplier/refiner automatically. No pending output may be manually claimed twice. Crash/save tests cover before/after transaction snapshots; cross-device rollback guarantees remain those of existing save reconciliation, not server-authoritative reward provenance.

XP/practice: ordinary cycle XP unchanged; discovery itself awards no extra XP. Projects award listed XP once on successful committed completion, and nominal base-work seconds to their task mastery (capped normally). Cancelled/blocked work earns no completion XP. Project mastery grants speed only; no protected yield or conservation. Old ordinary action mastery IDs remain intact; new tasks use `task.<actionId>`, project tracks `task.project_<projectId>`, Heist tracks `task.heist.<jobId>`, matching the audited generators. Bulk recipes keep their single-recipe track.

Live/offline share award functions and event ordering. Parity means equal settings/RNG/work with no manual actions and no intentional offline-speed bonus; an offline specialization is allowed its existing speed advantage. Summaries show gross ordinary output, discoveries, protected progress, completed projects, net spending and exact stop reason separately. Merge summaries by counts and discovery sets, not replayed claims. Show remaining work and source links on every project.

## 4. Content conventions and dependency graph

All new ordinary gather recipes have no input/station unless stated. Rare accelerators are 1% per completed foreground cycle, one extra fragment, unless a row overrides it; they are not required. Deterministic counters persist across changing tasks and reload, reset only when paying their threshold reward, and accrue from the named action. Greenhouse can produce the same named bloom/material at its existing slower rate; discoveries never count bonus/manual cycles.

Gathering tier ladder for **new** content uses levels 45/60/75/90, intervals 5/6/7/8 seconds and final XP 18/24/30/36 respectively. Existing level-1/15/30 actions retain 3/3.5/4 seconds and 2.1/5.4/11.4 final XP. New refining uses matching tier interval and XP 8/12/16/20 for those four tiers; old bar/plank recipes retain their source values. These are local action rates, not a new global XP curve. Higher tiers add uses and progress, not a promise that a fresh level-1 skiller reaches 90 overnight.

Supplier graph, excluding optional trade:

`Mining -> bars, fossils, rough gems, mineral fragments -> Smithing / Gemcutting / Rune-carving`;
`Woodcutting -> logs, planks, resin, dust, heartwood -> Smithing / Alchemy / Cartography / Rune-carving`;
`Foraging -> herbs, essences, blooms -> Alchemy / Cooking / Rune-carving`;
`Thieving marks -> gold, Markers -> stations / crew / Heist`;
`Archaeology -> artifacts, fragments -> restorations / merchant supplies`;
`Cartography -> its own sheets/inks -> plain Maps / Sextants / unique charts`.

Alchemy, Cooking, Gemcutting and Rune-carving contribute late project components but never gate starter suppliers or station 3. Each below has a level-1 recipe. Every ingredient in the project matrix (§7) has an explicit source here or in the combat hook table (§8). Finished unique recipes stay hidden/disabled until their phase implements the actual effect; supplier materials may exist earlier with “future project” use text.

## 5. Eleven profession contracts

### 5.1 Mining — Phase 02

Retain `mine_copper`, all six existing fossil seam/vein IDs, `mine_iron`, `mine_gem`, `mine_mithril` and their old ordinary rewards/rates; remove areas. Add prospecting accomplishments at 100 completed cycles of the preceding ordinary tier, IDs `prospect_iron`, `prospect_mithril`, `prospect_adamant`, `prospect_star`, `prospect_void`, `prospect_world`. Existing iron/mithril actions remain level-only for compatibility; new tiers need the preceding prospect accomplishment. At level 30, 100 mithril cycles discovers adamant. At each new tier 100 cycles discovers the next. No rare drop or mastery requirement.

New actions `mine_adamant`, `mine_star`, `mine_void`, `mine_world` at ladder levels produce `adamant_ore`, `star_ore`, `void_ore`, `world_ore` respectively (one/cycle). For each metal in `{iron,mithril,adamant,star,void,world}` expand three specialized actions at that metal's level and prospect requirement:

| ID expansion | Seconds / final XP | Guaranteed output | Progress/optional output |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mine_rich_<metal>` | 1.5 × ordinary interval / 1.5 × ordinary XP | metal ore 2 | no rough gem or fragment; focused metal |
| `mine_crystal_<metal>` | 2 × interval / 2 × XP | rough_gem 1 | ore every second completion; `crystal_shard` P every 30 cycles; 1% extra shard |
| `mine_fossil_<metal>` | 1.5 × interval / 1.5 × XP | ore every second completion | deterministic rotating fossil every 12 cycles; no ordinary rough-gem roll |

Fossil rotation is exactly `scorched_fossil, frigid_fossil, metallic_fossil, serrated_fossil, dense_fossil`, persisted index; preview next output/count. This gives at least one chosen fossil in 60 cycles. Existing random fossil drops remain accelerators. `mine_gem` is the dependable low-level rough-gem route; no Mining input needs crafted gems.

At mithril or later ordinary/crystal/fossil deposits, grant `mineral_fragment` P every 60 cycles; rich deposits omit it. At adamant+ add `stone_fragment` P every 60 cycles, separate counter. 1% extra corresponding fragment per eligible cycle; world ore has both. Special mineral and stone fragments feed alloys, tools and uniques. Lower copper/iron remain in alloys and station costs. Phase acceptance: independent old/fresh miners can reach every tier, choose yield vs fossil/crystal progress, and zero-luck fragments appear at the advertised bound.

### 5.2 Woodcutting — Phase 03

Keep ash/oak/yew, ley/weeping and milling IDs, ordinary inputs/yields/XP and bonuses, remove areas. Add `cut_ironwood`, `cut_ancient`, `cut_spirit`, `cut_worldwood` at ladder levels producing `ironwood_log`, `ancient_log`, `spirit_log`, `worldwood_log`. Unlock each by its level plus 100 cycles of preceding ordinary wood (`wood_<wood>_survey`); no Mining/Smithing gate.

For `{ash,oak,yew,ironwood,ancient,spirit,worldwood}`, `cut_resin_<wood>` at that wood's level uses 1.5 × ordinary interval/XP: log every second cycle plus `<wood>_resin` guaranteed every 5 cycles. Existing resin actions keep their random bonuses. `mill_<newWood>`: matching refinement interval/XP, 5 logs -> 1 `<wood>_plank`. `cut_ley` remains an unconditional, 1 dust/cycle source at level 8. New resins add item levels only as follows: ironwood 3, ancient 4, spirit 5, worldwood 6; respect Smithing cap and preserve existing resin definitions.

`cut_heartwood` at Woodcutting 30 and `woodcutting_work_100`: 8 seconds / 12 XP, yew_log 1 every second completion, `heartwood_fragment` P every 30 completions, 1% extra fragment. This is an independently selected ancient-tree survey, not a random yew drop. `mill_heartwood`: level 30, 30 seconds / 30 XP, 20 fragments -> `ancient_heartwood` P 1. One heartwood is bounded by 80 minutes of cutting + 30 seconds of milling from zero. Ancient+ ordinary trees also give a fragment every 60 cycles; the survey remains the faster targeted route. Heartwood frames support bows/staves/defensive projects; final equipment stays in Smithing.

### 5.3 Smithing and tools — Phase 04

Keep all `smith_<baseId>` base recipes and catalyst behavior; use new strength rule in §3. Add `smelt_adamant`, `smelt_star`, `smelt_void`, `smelt_world`: level/interval/XP per refinement ladder, 5 corresponding ores -> 1 bar. Ordinary smelting has no Forge requirement beyond default 1.

New component recipes (each output P, no cost conservation):

| ID | Level / station | Seconds / XP | Input -> output |
|---|---|---|---|
| `alloy_tempered` | 15 / Forge 2 | 15 / 18 | iron_bar 2, copper_bar 1 -> tempered_alloy 1 |
| `alloy_wound` | 60 / Forge 3 | 60 / 120 | mithril_bar 5, copper_bar 2, mineral_fragment 10 -> wound_alloy 1 |
| `alloy_stone` | 70 / Forge 3 | 60 / 150 | adamant_bar 5, iron_bar 2, stone_fragment 10 -> stone_alloy 1 |
| `frame_heartwood` | 45 / Forge 3 | 60 / 90 | ancient_heartwood 1, yew_plank 5 -> heartwood_frame 1 |
| `frame_convergence` | 85 / Forge 3 | 120 / 300 | wound_alloy 1, stone_alloy 1, ancient_heartwood 1 -> convergence_frame 1 |

**Targeted equipment:** finite expansion `purpose_<purpose>_<baseId>` for every obtainable nonretired base whose tags can hold the selected guarantee. Level = base reqLevel, station = current base station rule; duration `60 + 2 * reqLevel` seconds, final XP `18 + 1.8 * reqLevel`. Inputs = 2 × the matching existing cheap-base inputs, plus 2 corresponding tier bars (copper below 10, iron below 22, mithril otherwise). Wood-consuming bases additionally require one matching plank. No all-profession chain for an ordinary upgrade.

Four purposes: `physical` requires attack weapon and guarantees `added_phys` + `inc_phys`; `protection` requires armour/offhand and guarantees `flat_life` + the matching existing local defence affix (`inc_armour` for pure STR, `inc_evasion` for pure DEX, `inc_armour_evasion` for STR/DEX, `inc_armour_es` for STR/INT, `inc_evasion_es` for DEX/INT; pure INT excluded from this purpose); `resistance` requires a compatible armour/jewellery base, selectable `fire`, `cold` or `light` subtype in the ID (`purpose_resistance_<element>_<baseId>`), guarantees `flat_life` + `<element>_res`; `resource` guarantees `flat_mana` + `spell_damage` on caster weapons, `es_and_mana` + `inc_es` on pure INT armour, `es_and_mana` + the corresponding hybrid local ES defence affix on INT hybrids, and `flat_mana` + `mana_regen` on jewellery. These are existing literal affix IDs, not new families. If both guarantees do not have positive eligible weight on a base, its recipe is absent, not silently weakened. On hybrid bases, the INT resource purpose takes precedence over protection only when the player selects that purpose; neither changes the other's recipe.

Output one rare, exactly four explicit mods: the two guarantees plus one compatible remaining prefix and suffix when capacity permits; never duplicate groups. Each guaranteed affix uses the best existing tier eligible at `max(base.reqLevel, resultingIlvl - 10)` and rolls uniformly in its top half (integer round up). Fillers roll existing eligible pools normally. No T0/elevated/fractured/doom result. Preview concrete minimum values before start. Output stops after one by default and ignores salvage/filter settings. Missing optional resin/fossil must not alter guarantees; exclude those slots from this recipe. Reason: two assured useful stats make the extra gathering time worthwhile without replacing the whole orb/bench system.

**Early exclusive project:** `tool_wayfarer_hammer`, Smithing 15, Forge 2, `smithing_work_20`; 10 minutes / 180 XP; copper_bar 10, iron_bar 10, ash_plank 5, oak_plank 5 -> one fixed magic profession tool in the existing weapon slot, no combat requirements beyond level 1. Effect: 8% increased Smithing speed, no combat damage bonus. New base `profession_hammer` has weapon tags, 1–2 physical damage, 0 speed/crit and no implicit. This is a profession project, not a fourteenth roster unique; rarity magic, fixed protected explicit, normal one-rune slot. No extra equipment/tool slot. It is craftable without later-phase components and does not replace any accepted unique.

**Other profession tools:** `tool_<skill>` for the remaining ten saved profession IDs, level 30 of owner skill, `<skill>_work_100`, station default 1 except Smithing's hammer, 20 minutes / 360 XP. Cost copper_bar 10, mithril_bar 10, oak_plank 5, rough_gem 5; each gives fixed 8% speed only for its named profession, on the same `profession_hammer` statless tool base. Names in saved-skill order: Prospector's Pick (Mining), Heartwood Hatchet (Woodcutting), Seedkeeper's Sickle (`harvesting`), Quiet Hand (Thieving), Restorer's Trowel (Archaeology), Distiller's Stirrer (Alchemy), Lapidary's Chisel (Gemcutting), Inscriber’s Stylus (Rune-carving), Hearth Spoon (Cooking), Surveyor's Pen (Cartography). This includes a Cooking utensil; secondary to food. Add each tool only in its profession's phase. They share the existing weapon slot and cannot stack with another tool; no combat balance bonuses. Materials have independent suppliers; another profession is allowed to make the raw bars, not required to complete the tool project. Gear switching uses existing rules and Bench requirement, not a new free automation. New fixed tool affix IDs are `profession_speed_<skill>`; they never enter random affix pools. Tools may be traded under existing equipment policy; unlocks are not transferred.

### 5.4 Foraging/Greenhouse — Phase 06

Keep all `herb_*` IDs and existing herb/essence rolls, remove areas; saved skill remains `harvesting`. Herbs keep identities: sunpetal recovery, frostleaf mana/protection, emberroot damage/life brews, voidbloom utility/high brewing. Each existing primary herb gets `herb_abundant_<herb>` at its ordinary action level, 1.5 × base interval/XP -> herb 2, no essence/bloom progress. `herb_concentrated_<herb>` at ordinary level + 5: 2 × base interval/XP -> herb 1 plus deterministic essence on every 12 cycles. Rotation: sunpetal = vitality_1; frostleaf = frost_2/vitality_2 alternating; emberroot = embers_2/storm_2 alternating; voidbloom = haste_2. These IDs are prefixed `essence_` in the actual material registry.
