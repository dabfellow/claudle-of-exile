# Acts 4–6

Decided 2026-10-31. Act 4's stages are built, in [ACT4.md](ACT4.md); the rest isn't yet. It's Milestone 7 of [MILESTONES.md](../MILESTONES.md), planned from the
checklist in [ACT_RECIPE.md](ACT_RECIPE.md). The names and places come from the continent map.

## Smaller calls

| | Act 4: The Hollow Peaks | Act 5: The Sunless Sea | Act 6: The Shattered Throne |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stages** | 12 | 14 | 18 |
| **Monster levels** | 41–49 | 49–57 | 57–65 |
| **Resistance check** | Cold | Fire and lightning together | All three |
| **Ascendancy trial** | Last stage: opens notables | – | First stage: opens the capstone |
| **Ground rule added** | Frozen | Storm-tossed water, or as the theme needs | Unholy ground |

- **Resistance checks** get harder rather than just changing colour. Act 4 repeats cold at a higher level, Act 5 needs two
  at once, and Act 6 asks for all three, so the end of the campaign tests the whole build.
- **Rewards:** bases up to about level 80 start dropping across these acts, plus 6 new uniques each.
- **Ending:** beating Act 6 opens the Map Device and unlocks Exile for the account.
- **Fishing is never being added.** The "Soon" entry in the menu is a Path of Exile joke, and it stays.

## Act 4: The Hollow Peaks

- **Place, the giants' range:** huge frozen peaks where giants and beasts rule, and you're small. Monsters are large and
  few, so it plays as a change from swarms.
- **Boss mechanic, Frostbite:** stacks build on you as the fight goes on, and slow you until you freeze solid. By hand, you
  cleanse. By rule, a skill or flask fires at a stack count.
- **Skill batch, Giants and weight:** huge slow hits, stuns and shields, a heavy slow style the game doesn't have yet. It
  suits the Warrior and Templar.
- **New skill, Archaeology, feeding Expedition:** dig sites in the peaks for Artifacts to trade for currency. It needs no
  inputs.

## Act 5: The Sunless Sea

- **Place, the drowned dark:** a sea under permanent storm and shadow, crossed island to island by ship. Monsters are deep
  things and the crews they've taken. It answers Act 1's Drowned Coast, as this is where the drowning came from.
- **Boss mechanic, Channels:** the boss starts a long, growing spell that you must break. By hand, you hit it with a stun
  or interrupt in time. By rule, a skill fires on a channel. It teaches rules to tell a channel from a slam, which
  [HARD_BOSSES.md](HARD_BOSSES.md) needs.
- **Skill batch, Tides and summons:** minions called from the deep, and storms that stay on the field.
- **New skill, Cooking:** gathered plants become strong flask ingredients and food. It's a smaller skill with no new system
  of its own, which is a deliberate exception to the recipe.

## Act 6: The Shattered Throne

- **Place, the king who broke the world:** what's left of the empire that exiled you, torn apart when its last king reached
  for something he shouldn't have. It sets up The Crownless, the last hard mode boss.
- **Boss mechanic, Echoes:** bosses bring back mechanics from earlier acts, two at a time, so a boss might scorch the ground
  while its martyrs stand.
- **Skill batch, The last empire:** the strongest skills in the game, two signature skills per attribute, with big
  rule-bending supports.
- **New skill, Cartography, feeding Maps:** draw and upgrade maps, trained during Act 6, so it's ready when the Map Device
  opens.
