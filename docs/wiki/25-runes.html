Runes

Rune-carving opens at Pilgrim Gate in Act 3. A carved rune is kept in the shared materials bank, then inscribed into the one rune slot on a piece of gear.

One rune, three readings

A rune's effect depends on where you put it: weapons use the Weapon reading; rings, amulets and belts use Jewellery; every other gear slot uses Armour. The four lines in each cell below are Scratched, Carved, Deep and Sainted.

Grades and item level

Higher grades need more Rune-carving levels and can only go on an item with enough item level. Families within a grade unlock two Rune-carving levels apart, so the level shown is when the first family in that grade opens.

Resonance

Equip more than one rune from the same family to create Resonance. Each other matching equipped piece makes every rune in that family {{data:rune-resonance}}% stronger. Different grades can resonate. Deep Cuts makes all equipped runes 10% stronger; Living Script doubles the Resonance part but makes Rune-carving 25% slower.

Before you inscribe

  • Runes work on Normal, Magic, Rare and Unique gear.
  • Inscribe consumes the rune. Replacing a rune destroys the old one.
  • Orbs, Wax and ordinary modifier crafting do not change or restore a rune.
  • A rune's bonus is global even when it is on a weapon or armour; it is not part of that item's local modifiers.
