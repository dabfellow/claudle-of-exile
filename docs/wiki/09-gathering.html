Gathering and production

Life skills run instead of fighting, one job at a time, and level up separately from your character. Gathering skills collect materials from nothing. Production skills turn materials into gear, flasks, gems and more. All eleven professions can be started without campaign progress. Higher-tier ordinary tasks require skill levels, materials and sometimes a station upgrade. Equipment and combat encounters keep their own requirements.

Smithing an upgrade

Cheap bases keep their existing catalyst and salvage options. Choose an Equipment purpose to make a rare with two guaranteed useful modifiers: Physical (added and increased physical damage), Protection (Life and local defence), a chosen elemental Resistance (Life and that resistance), or Resource (Mana/spell damage, Energy Shield/Mana and local ES defence, or jewellery Mana/regeneration). Only compatible bases appear. Expand a recipe to see its exact minimum rolls, costs and supply count before starting.

Purpose gear has four modifiers: the two guarantees and one further compatible prefix and suffix. Guarantees use the best tier at the greater of the base requirement or item level minus ten, rolling in that tier's top half. It ignores fossils, resins and salvage settings, needs a free bag slot, and stops after one. Other orb and bench crafting still works. Smithing level and Forge progress determine strength: Forge 1/2/3+ cap item level at 21/45/90. Character level and attributes are still required to equip.

Refine five ores per bar, including Adamant/Star/Void/World at Smithing 45/60/75/90. Separately select Tempered Alloy (15/Forge 2), Wound Alloy (60/Forge 3), Stone Alloy (70/Forge 3), Heartwood Frame (45/Forge 3) or Convergence Frame (85/Forge 3). Components pay full listed costs and produce exactly one protected material per cycle, without conservation or doubled output. Alloys and frames are stock for later projects; finished unique recipes remain unavailable. Ordinary purpose gear only needs its displayed ore, bars, wood and any Rough Gems.

If you can't make something yet

  • Short of materials: gather them, or check whether a boss drops them. Bosses have a 50% chance to drop a little gathering material.
  • Skill too low: train it on a cheaper recipe or lower node.
  • Missing an accomplishment: complete the stated profession work; mastery is a bonus and never an access requirement.
  • Forge or Alchemy too low: upgrade the station in your Hideout. See Unlocks and progression.

Gathering spots

Mining deposits and prospecting

Ordinary Copper → Iron → Mithril → Adamant → Star → Void → World veins survey the next metal after 100 completions. Levels are 1/15/30/45/60/75/90. Existing Iron/Mithril veins keep level-only access; specialized deposits require their metal's prospecting discovery too. All access is independent of combat and mastery.

Rich deposits give two ore in 1.5 times ordinary work, with no gems or fragments. Crystal deposits take twice ordinary work and give one Rough Gem each cycle, one ore every second cycle and a protected Crystal Shard every 30 crystal cycles. Fossil deposits take 1.5 times ordinary work, give ore every second cycle and rotate Scorched, Frigid, Metallic, Serrated and Dense fossils every 12 fossil cycles: one of each in 60. Fossils already steer orb/Smithing crafts and supply Rune-carving; Rough Gems already supply gem cuts and jewellery.

Mithril+ ordinary/crystal/fossil deposits give a protected Mineral Fragment every 60 eligible cycles; Adamant+ also give Stone Fragments every 60. Each eligible fragment has a separate 1% extra chance. Counters survive changing eligible deposits and reloads; rich deposits do not advance them. Protected fragments cannot multiply with mastery, yield bonuses or manual doubles. Materials have no storage-slot limit.

Advanced ores refine into Smithing bars. Mineral and Stone Fragments feed Wound and Stone Alloy, then Convergence Frames. Crystal shards remain stock for later exceptional-crystal cutting. Finished unique projects are unavailable. Copper and iron retain base/construction uses and feed alloys, purpose gear and tools. Nothing starts the next refining task automatically.

Foraging patches, essences and blooms

Herbs keep their distinct purposes: Sunpetal for recovery flasks and meals; Frostleaf for mana and protection brews; Emberroot for damage/life brews; and Voidbloom for utility and high brewing. Each primary herb has an Abundant Patch (1.5× time, two herbs, no essence/seed progress) and a Concentrated Patch (2× time, one herb, plus a guaranteed rotating essence every 12 completions). Sunpetal yields Vitality Essence I; Frostleaf rotates Frost/Vitality II; Emberroot rotates Embers/Storm II; Voidbloom yields Haste II. Emberbloom Bed also adds guaranteed Storm/Embers I essences alternating every 12 completions alongside its random rolls.

Moon Bloom (level 45, 100 Foraging cycles) yields Frostleaf and one protected Moon Bloom every 60 cycles plus 1% extra. Star Bloom (level 60, 100 Foraging cycles) yields Voidbloom and one protected Star Bloom every 60 cycles plus 1% extra. Each plant has a seed discovery (after 100 completions at its source, or 1% chance per foreground cycle) that unlocks explicit planting in the Greenhouse once its keystone is allocated.

XP per hour assumes no speed bonuses. Spots with a yield below 100% trade their main material for rarer finds.

Saved projects

Foraging 30 and 100 completed Foraging cycles open the Seedkeeper's Sickle project: 10 Copper Bars, 10 Mithril Bars, 5 Oak Planks and 5 Rough Gems, then 20 minutes / 360 XP. It awards a fixed magic tool with 8% increased Foraging speed in the existing main-hand weapon slot (level 1 to equip, one normal rune slot). Woodcutting 30 opens the Heartwood Hatchet (8% Woodcutting speed) under the same terms.

Smithing 15, Forge 2 and 20 completed Smithing cycles open Wayfarer's Hammer: 10 Copper Bars, 10 Iron Bars, 5 Ash Planks and 5 Oak Planks, then 10 minutes / 180 XP. This fixed magic main-hand profession tool gives 8% Smithing speed only, requires level 1 to equip, and keeps one normal rune slot. It uses full protected payment and safe project delivery. With level-15 suppliers and Forge 2 ready, gathering, refinement and assembly take about 21 minutes of base work; initial leveling and station purchases are additional.

Mining 30 and 100 completed Mining cycles open the Prospector's Pick project: 10 Copper Bars, 10 Mithril Bars, 5 Oak Planks and 5 Rough Gems, then 20 minutes / 360 XP. It awards a fixed magic tool with 8% increased Mining speed in the existing main-hand weapon slot (level 1 to equip). No combat bonus, no tool stacking or ordinary modifier crafting; its normal one-rune slot remains. Raw bars/planks can be supplied by other professions. Protected payment/delivery rules below apply.

Copper Supply on the Smithing page sets aside 25 Copper Ore for 15 seconds and awards five Copper Bars and 12 XP. It uses the same base cost and work as five normal smelts. Protected projects do not double outputs or conserve inputs. Their mastery improves speed only.

Starting a different activity or pressing Stop pauses a project. Resume keeps the paid inputs and progress. Cancel discards partial work and returns exactly what was reserved, once. Finished equipment waits safely if your inventory is full. Repeats must be selected explicitly and stop when supplies run out; they never start gathering ingredients for you. Return summaries separate ordinary production, protected progress, discoveries, completed projects and net spending.
