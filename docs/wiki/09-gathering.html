Gathering and production

Life skills run instead of fighting, one job at a time, and level up separately from your character. Gathering skills collect materials from nothing. Production skills turn materials into gear, flasks, gems and more. Higher-tier spots open as you level the skill and reach certain areas.

If you can't make something yet

  • Short of materials: gather them, or check whether a boss drops them. Bosses have a 50% chance to drop a little gathering material.
  • Skill too low: train it on a cheaper recipe or lower node.
  • Spot not open: the table below names the area that opens it.
  • Forge or Alchemy too low: upgrade the station in your Hideout. See Unlocks and progression.
