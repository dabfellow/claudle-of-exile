Getting started

Path of Idle is an idle action RPG. Your character fights on their own, keeps fighting while you are away (up to a limit), and you decide what they wear, which skills they use and when those skills fire.

Your first minutes

  1. Open the Campaign tab and enter your first area, The Mud Flats. Old Brannoc, the guide, walks you through the first steps.
  2. Your Default Attack swings by itself. Skills wait for you: click one, or give it a rule on the Skills page so it fires on its own.
  3. Drink your Life Flask before you are in trouble, not after. Flasks refill as you kill.
  4. Equip better gear as it drops. See Item bases and Modifiers to learn what is worth keeping.

Areas and bosses

Each area has a boss that appears after a set number of kills. Beating it opens the road onward. The last boss of an act finishes the act. Boss fights have special moves, listed per area on Acts and areas.

Automation

Skills can run on rules: for example, "use this when life is low". Simple rules are free. More reactive rules, such as ones that watch enemy status or boss wind-ups, are earned through the game, and a new character cannot idle bosses until it has them.

Offline play

Your character keeps fighting while you are away, up to an offline limit that starts at 8 hours and can be raised. Offline fighting stops if your character dies.

Classes

Pick a class that matches how you want to play. Each has its own combat mechanic, and later a choice of ascendancy. See Classes and ascendancies.
