Report incorrect information

Found a mistake on this wiki, or a bug in the game? Please tell us.

  1. Open the game.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Find the Feedback card and choose Bug.
  4. Include the wiki page's address, what it says, and what you think is right.

Your note goes straight to the developer. New reports include your character name, equipment, skill priority and linked supports, passives, movement posture, combat state and ten recent combat log lines. Very large snapshots may be partial. Authentication tokens and browser storage are not attached.

Signed-in reports are linked to your account so administrators can identify you, inspect the relevant save and reply. Your account email and diagnostic state are visible only to administrators. Older reports may have only version, class, level and area information.

Check Settings for developer replies. If compensation is sent, open a character in the named realm and collect it there.
