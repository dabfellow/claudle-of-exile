Farming strategies

There's no single best place to farm. The right choice depends on what you need and what you can survive. These are suggestions, so test them with your own character.

Farming XP

Stay inside an area's level range. Its page lists the range, and the Campaign shows when your XP is changed.

  • Below the range: +10% XP at one level under, +20% at two or more.
  • Above the range: −10%, −20%, −40%, −50% and −80% for one to five levels over, falling further after that.

Moving up an area as soon as you can survive it usually beats clearing a safer area too long.

Farming orbs and items

Deeper areas shift the odds towards rarer orbs and better rarity. Loot and drop tables lists the rates. Item Rarity raises your unique chance, and raises your currency chance by a third as much.

Farming a unique

Uniques drop in set areas. Find the item's page, open its drop list, and farm there. Bosses have a far better chance per kill than normal monsters, but take longer to reach.

Farming bosses

A boss guarantees its reward on the first kill, then gives it only sometimes. So beat each boss once for its reward, even if you farm somewhere else. The chances are in the boss table.

Farming materials

Gathering is the steady source. If you want both gear and materials, consider fighting while you're around and gathering while you're away, or the other way round, depending on which is safer for your character.

Choosing an activity to leave running

  • Not sure you'll survive? Pick gathering. It has no monsters, so nothing can end it early but running out of materials.
  • Need levels? Fight in the highest area you clear safely.
  • Need an upgrade? Check the upgrade's cost first, then gather exactly that.
