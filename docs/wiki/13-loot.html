Loot and drop tables

The real drop rates from the game. Rates are per kill, and smaller monsters in a pack drop currency, items and flasks less often. Uniques only drop in areas that list them.

Item Rarity multiplies your unique chance, and affects currency chance a third as much. So 30% Item Rarity gives 1.3 times the unique chance and 1.1 times the currency chance.

Orbs that only come from fighting

Exalted, Divine and Annulment Orbs remain combat loot. Regals can also come from Heist, and Expedition merchants sell selected currencies such as Chaos and Instilling Orbs with standing/stock limits. Monster Instilling Orb drops start in Act 4.
