How some rules read the fight

  • When an enemy winds up watches for slams and novas, not channels. When an enemy channels covers those.
  • When enemies at least doesn't count chained martyrs.
  • Target rules read the monster your last skill went for.
  • Ignite as a status also counts a burn.
  • When its effect is not active checks the skill's own buff, shield, minions, storm, or its ailment or curse on the target.

Why didn't this skill fire?

Go down this list. A skill must clear all of it.

  1. Its rule is Manual only. That's how every gem starts.
  2. One of its rules failed. With several rules, all must pass.
  3. A rule is still locked. A locked rule never passes.
  4. It's on cooldown.
  5. You can't pay. It needs the mana, including any Mana reserved for eligible higher-priority skills due within 1.5 seconds. A Life cost must also leave you {{data:life-reserve}}% of your Life when a rule fires it, though your own press can go lower.
  6. Wrong weapon. The skill's page lists what it needs. A spell can't be used with a sword, for example.
  7. Level or attribute too low. Gems need a character level and an attribute.
  8. Nothing in reach. Damage skills need a target in range, and melee skills reach ring 1. Your character closes in if there's a clear way.
  9. Another skill went first. See which skill goes first, and raise its priority.
  10. A support took over. Skills with Reprisal or Succession fire on what those supports say, whatever their rules are.
