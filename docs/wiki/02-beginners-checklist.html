Beginner's checklist

Work down this list with a new character. It follows Old Brannoc's tutorial, then carries on through the first act.

First session

  • Enter {{area:mudflats}} and kill a few monsters.
  • Drink your Life Flask and your Mana Flask once each.
  • Give your main skill the rule Whenever ready on the Skills page.
  • Mine Copper Ore and smith a weapon of the kind you already hold.
  • Use an {{currency:transmute}} on it and equip it.
  • Open your Hideout and build the Crafting Bench, then salvage an item.
  • Allocate your first passive point.
  • Beat {{monster:gorger}}.

Before you log off

  • Pick an area you can clear safely. A death ends offline fighting.
  • Check every skill on your bar has a rule, or it will never fire while you're away.
  • Put your bag in order. Offline finds wait in their own pile, which holds 200 items.

First upgrades

  • Use the free Vitals, Wind-up and Ground rules immediately. War Table upgrades add advanced rules and loot automation.
  • Upgrade the Watchtower for 12 hours offline.
  • Put a guard skill on When an enemy winds up, and a heal on When life below.
  • Keep your resistances up as you reach elemental bosses.
  • Check station costs: upgrades use refined bars/planks and Boss Trophies as well as Gold. Refine the listed inputs rather than stockpiling raw logs and ore blindly.

Finishing Act 1

  • Stay near the area's level. Past the top of its range, kill XP falls quickly.
  • Read each boss's page before you fight it.
  • Beat {{monster:ysolde}} to open Act 2.
