Maps, Atlas and Sextants

Maps are the repeatable endgame after Act 6. The Last King tops your realm up to at least five Tier 1 Maps. Each run consumes one map, borrows the look, monsters and boss mechanics of a campaign area, clears a fixed number of packs, then ends with its boss.

Map tiers

{{data:map-tiers}}

Maps found inside a map normally match the current tier, with a base 15% chance to be one tier higher when a higher tier exists. You can run one map or auto-run the same tier until its shared supply runs out. Each new normal map fully restores Life, Mana, Energy Shield and flask charges. Leaving or dying consumes the map and stops auto-run unless the Atlas's Unbroken Path refunds a normal map after death.

Optional normal-map modifiers

Normal maps can be left plain or set to roll two or three extra modifiers on entry. Blacklist modifiers you do not want; automatic rerolling spends one Chaos Orb per attempt, up to five attempts for that run, and stops early if you run out.

Atlas points and tree

The Atlas is shared by the realm. Each normal tier can award three points once: one for its first clear, one for a deathless clear and one for clearing with every unlocked Sextant slot filled. A first clear of each Doomed tier gives 2; first Hard Boss clears give 3 each and first Uber Boss clears give 5 each. Earnings stop at {{data:atlas-cap}} points.

The {{data:atlas-nodes}}-node tree has Sustain, Bounty, Surveying and Peril wings. You may begin from any wing and each later node needs a connected predecessor. Refund one unneeded leaf or reset the whole Atlas for free. Doomed Horizon needs 20 Atlas points earned and lets Tier 8-10 bosses drop Doomed Maps.

Sextants

Apply a Sextant to an unlocked Map Device slot to roll one modifier. It normally lasts {{data:sextant-runs}} applicable map runs; Atlas Surveying nodes and the highest Map Device upgrade can add charges. Apprentice modifiers work on Tier 1+, Journeyman on Tier 5+ and Master on Tier 8+. A charge is only spent when the map tier is high enough for that modifier.

Doomed Maps

Doomed Maps I-V are separate, harder maps. They always include elite packs, roll their listed number of unique Doomed modifiers and do not use Sextants or normal-map modifiers. They may drop Fractured Orbs, and their bosses have the listed chance to drop a random Uber key.

{{data:doomed-tiers}} {{data:doomed-mods}}

Your modifier blacklist and Chaos reroll setting also apply to Doomed modifiers, with the same limit of five paid rerolls. Doomed Maps normally refill Life, Mana and Energy Shield, but Drained belts starts every flask empty. Doomed maps are not refunded by Unbroken Path.
