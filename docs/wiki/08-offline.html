Offline progression

When you're away for at least a minute, the game plays out that time when you return, then shows a summary of what happened. It runs the same fight or job you left going. It doesn't guess at a result.

How long it lasts

Time past your limit is lost, so upgrade the Watchtower in your Hideout to raise it.

{{data:watchtower}}

What keeps going

  • Fighting, and everything it earns.
  • Gathering and production, while you have the materials.
  • Map runs, Heists and Expedition digs.
  • Auto-crafting, once the Crafting Bench is high enough.
  • A greenhouse, which keeps growing even after a job ends.

How it differs from playing live

  • Only rules fire. Nothing is pressed for you, and a press you queued before leaving is dropped.
  • Bosses may hide. See below.
  • Finds go to the offline haul. It holds {{data:haul-cap}} items, and the oldest fall off past that.
  • The skilling minigame's speed boost doesn't run.
  • Overtime, a skill tree node, makes skilling 10% faster offline.

Bosses while you're away

A boss only comes out offline if your rules could finish it. If not, it stays in hiding and you keep farming the area. The summary tells you when a boss was waiting.

  • Every boss: Wind-up rules are already unlocked. Configure useful defensive rules and make sure the build can survive.
  • Martyrs: needs a ruled skill that strikes them, either an area or chain skill or one aimed with Aim rules.
  • Frostbite: needs a guard or movement skill on the Frostbite rule, or a flask Instilled for it.
  • Channels: needs a skill on When an enemy channels that can interrupt or stun. On a boss with martyrs, it must interrupt.

What ends it

  • A death. Fighting stops for the rest of your time away.
  • Running out of materials for a job.
  • Reaching your limit.

Levels still help offline, but kill XP falls away quickly once you pass the top of an area's level range. That stops a long night outlevelling the campaign.

Preparing to leave

  • Pick an area you clear without trouble. It's better to farm safely all night than die in the first hour.
  • Give every skill a rule, and put your guard on a defensive rule.
  • Check you have enough materials for a long job, or use a batch recipe.
  • Pick up your loot first. A death marks what's on the ground, and a second death loses it.
  • Instill a flask for Low Life if you have the orb.
