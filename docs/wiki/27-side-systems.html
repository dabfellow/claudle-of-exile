Side systems

Life skills have two kinds of long-term specialisation: the Life Skills tree, which you choose, and work mastery, which grows automatically as you repeat a kind of work. Heists and Expedition add their own longer loops.

Life Skills tree

You earn one point for every 10 total levels across the eleven real life skills, up to {{data:skill-point-cap}} points. Fishing does not count. The tree cannot be fully completed, so choose the branches that support the work you actually do. Branch Notables require level 15 and 30 in that skill; its Keystone requires level 50. Refunds cost an Orb of Regret and must leave the tree connected.

Work mastery

Finishing an eligible timed action adds its unmodified base time to that work band. Live play, offline play, repeats and Greenhouse completions all count; interrupted work does not. Each band reaches level 100 after {{data:mastery-cap-hours}} hours of nominal practice.

  • Every band gains up to {{data:mastery-speed}} percentage points of speed.
  • Gathering bands gain up to {{data:mastery-yield}}% expected extra ordinary primary yield.
  • Production bands gain up to {{data:mastery-conserve}}% expected conservation of listed ordinary inputs.
  • Heist mastery gives speed only. Rare rewards, keys, maps and access requirements are never multiplied or bypassed.

Heists

Thieving earns Rogue's Markers. Spend them to run a Heist, choosing in advance how many rooms to attempt. Later rooms pay more and have a higher alarm chance; only the final room rolls the vault reward. An alarm ends the job and normally keeps {{data:heist-keeps}}% of your take. Extra Thieving levels reduce alarm chance, but only as far as {{data:heist-stealth-floor}}% of the job's normal chance.

Crew slots open at Thieving levels {{data:crew-slots}}. Rogues can reach rank {{data:rogue-rank-cap}}; each rank stacks its listed effect.

Archaeology and Expedition

Archaeology opens in Hollow Pass in Act 4. Sites never run out. Long-term finds occur after {{data:expedition-hours}} base-speed hours at a site and move it through {{data:expedition-strata}}. Each find permanently raises that site's artifact yield by 25% and gives one standing with its culture.

{{data:expedition}}

Spend each culture's Artifacts at its merchant. Better wares need standing and shelves refill over time. Standing orders buy again after restocks while you can afford them; they do not create stock or bypass standing.
