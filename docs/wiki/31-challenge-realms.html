Challenge realms

Hardcore, Solo Self-Found and Classic are permanent choices made when a character is created. You may combine them. The combination decides which shared realm that character uses.

What a realm shares

Characters in the same realm share the Hideout and station levels, Stash pages and overflow, banked currency and materials, spare gems and flasks, maps, Atlas progress, Sextants, automation unlocks and Unique discovery. Equipped gear, bag and ground items, levels, skill bars, passives, loadouts and current activity stay with the character.

Hardcore

A Hardcore death is final, including a death during offline combat. The character and everything it is carrying are lost. Shared-realm goods already in the stash or bank remain for other characters in that Hardcore realm. For Hardcore SSF, the private realm dies with its only character.

Solo Self-Found

SSF gives that character a fresh private realm: its own stash, Hideout, bank, maps and Atlas. It cannot use the Player Market. Other characters, even other SSF characters, do not share that realm.

Classic

Classic characters only fight. Gathering, production and the Life Skills tree are unavailable, but ordinary item crafting at the Crafting Bench still works. Hideout costs that would use gathered materials, refined components or trophies are converted to Gold. Bench recipes whose unlock normally requires skilling achievements are available because Classic cannot earn those achievements.

Combined modes

Non-SSF Hardcore, Classic and Hardcore Classic each have a separate shared realm for characters with that same combination. Standard characters share Standard only. Adding SSF always makes the realm private to that one character.
