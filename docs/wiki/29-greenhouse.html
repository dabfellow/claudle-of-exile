Greenhouse

Greenhouse is the level 50 Keystone at the end of the Foraging branch of the Life Skills tree. It lets your last selected Foraging spot keep producing at 20% speed while your character does something else.

Starting it

  1. Allocate the Greenhouse Keystone.
  2. Start any Foraging spot once. That becomes the remembered crop.
  3. Move to combat or another life skill. The remembered crop continues in the background.

Starting a different Foraging spot replaces the remembered crop. While you are actively Foraging, only the main Foraging action runs; Greenhouse does not add a second copy.

What applies

Greenhouse uses the normal action time and completes the same action, so its ordinary drops, rare finds, XP and work mastery all apply. Global gathering-speed events and the Overtime offline bonus also apply. The 20% background rate is separate from your ordinary Foraging speed.

Offline behavior

Greenhouse keeps growing offline and can continue even after combat stops from a death or another job runs out of materials. It still uses the normal offline time limit. If the remembered spot is no longer valid for your Foraging level, it waits instead of producing.
