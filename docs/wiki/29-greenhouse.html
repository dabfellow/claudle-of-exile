Greenhouse

Greenhouse is the Keystone at the end of the Foraging branch of the Life Skills tree. It lets a chosen or remembered Foraging crop keep producing at 20% speed in the background while your character does something else.

Starting it

  1. Allocate the Greenhouse Keystone in the Foraging skill tree using ordinary skilling points. No combat progression or station upgrade is required.
  2. Either select a learned plant from the Greenhouse dropdown on the Foraging page, or start any Foraging spot once. That becomes the remembered crop.
  3. Move to combat, crafting or another gathering skill. The crop continues growing in the background.

Starting a different Foraging spot replaces the remembered crop. While you are actively Foraging, only the main Foraging action runs; Greenhouse pauses and keeps its stored partial-cycle progress without adding a second copy.

Seeds and blooms

Each Foraging plant has a seed discovery (earned after 100 completions at its source, or a 1% chance per foreground cycle). Once discovered, that plant can be explicitly chosen in the Greenhouse. Moon Bloom and Star Bloom can also be planted; they advance their protected bloom counter at the 20% background rate. Grown and foraged blooms share the exact same material and are completely interchangeable in recipes.

What applies

Greenhouse uses the normal action time and completes the same action, so its ordinary drops, rare finds, XP and work mastery all apply. Global gathering-speed events and the Overtime offline bonus also apply. The 20% background rate is separate from your ordinary Foraging speed. Background growth does not roll early seed discoveries or double rewards.

Offline behavior

Greenhouse keeps growing offline and can continue even after combat stops from a death or another job runs out of materials. It still uses the normal offline time limit. If the remembered spot is no longer valid for your Foraging level, it waits instead of producing.
