Flasks and Instilling

Your belt has four fixed slots: one Life, one Mana and two Utility. Flasks refill from kills and are manual unless that flask has an Instilling enchantment.

Magic flask modifiers

A Magic flask can have one prefix and one suffix. Transmutation makes a Normal flask Magic, Augmentation adds a missing modifier, Alteration rerolls its modifiers, and Scouring returns it to Normal. Unique flasks cannot be modified this way.

Instilling Orbs

An Instilling Orb adds one automatic-use condition and replaces any previous condition. It does not change the flask's ordinary modifiers. The flask still needs enough charges when its condition happens.

{{data:instill}}

Frostbite Instilling becomes available with that Act 4 mechanic. Drinking any flask clears Frostbite; the automatic trigger fires at 6 stacks.

Practical choices

  • Low Life belongs on a Life flask; Low Mana belongs on a Mana flask.
  • Boss Wind-up is useful for defensive Utility flasks when you idle.
  • Full Charges keeps a Utility flask active often, but can spend charges before a dangerous moment.
  • New Pack is useful for an offensive or movement flask during ordinary farming.
