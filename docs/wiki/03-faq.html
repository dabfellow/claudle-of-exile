Frequently asked questions

Skills

Why didn't my skill fire?

The most common reasons are a Manual only rule, not enough mana, a cooldown, the wrong weapon, or no target in range. The troubleshooting list covers them all.

Can I cast spells with a dagger?

No. Spells need a wand, staff or sceptre in your main hand. Curses work with any weapon. Each skill's page lists the weapons it needs.

Why won't a support fit my skill?

A support fits by what the skill does. Pick your skill on the Support gems page to see exactly what fits it.

Dying

What do I lose when I die?

5% of the XP towards your next level, and never a level. A second death before you pick up your loot also loses the items that were on the ground at the first death.

What happens if a boss kills me?

It goes back into hiding, and you need to reach its kill count again to call it.

Offline

Why did my character stop while I was away?

Usually a death, or the job running out of materials. The summary when you return says what happened. See Offline progression.

Why didn't the boss appear offline?

Wind-up rules are available from the start. Later bosses can still wait offline if your bar lacks an automatic answer to martyrs, Frostbite or channels. See offline boss requirements.

Items and crafting

Where does this item drop?

Each item and unique has its own page, and uniques list their areas. Search for its name at the top of any page.

Which orbs can I only get by fighting?

Exalted, Divine and Annulment Orbs remain combat loot. Heists can award Regals; Expedition merchants also sell selected currencies, including Chaos and Instilling Orbs, subject to standing and stock. See Loot and drop tables.
