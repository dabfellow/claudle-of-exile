Builds and playstyles

A starting plan for each class. These are suggestions, not rules, and they haven't been played through as full builds. The build checks that every support listed really fits its skill, but the advice is opinion.

All of these use gems you can reach by about level 16. Gems come from the Town's Gem shop, which adds a few more with each area you open, so you may need to swap in what you have so far. Plan your own with the Build planner. Skills with a weapon limit are noted, so check before you commit.

Choosing an ascendancy

The first trial lets you pick one of your class's two ascendancies. Read both on Classes and ascendancies.

Each ascendancy has six notable branches and two capstones. The four trials award 2, 2, 3 and 2 points. A notable path costs two points and a capstone path costs three, so nine points support any three notables and either capstone. All branches are available after choosing your ascendancy; later trials award points rather than unlocking specific nodes.
