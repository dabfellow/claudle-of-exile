Builds and playstyles

A starting plan for each class. These are suggestions, not rules, and they haven't been played through as full builds. The build checks that every support listed really fits its skill, but the advice is opinion.

All of these use gems you can reach by about level 16. Gems come from the Town's Gem shop, with a few from Gemcutting, so you may need to swap in what you can get. Plan your own with the Build planner. Skills with a weapon limit are noted, so check before you commit.
