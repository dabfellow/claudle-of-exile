Independent professions

All eleven implemented life skills and the default Rune Board are available without advancing the campaign. Higher recipes still need their profession levels, ingredients and necessary stations. Classic characters remain combat-only. The Map Device still requires campaign completion, even if you have drawn maps already. Heist jobs unlock independently through Thieving levels and clean-exit milestones.

Forge 2/3 and Alchemy Table 2/3 have an alternative profession purchase route in the Hideout. Forge requires Smithing 10/22 and 20/60 completed cycles; the Table requires Alchemy 8/18 and 20/60 completed cycles. Gold comes from Thieving, bars from Smithing and planks from Woodcutting. The original purchase routes and higher upgrades remain available.

Smithed item level follows Smithing level plus Forge and resin bonuses, capped at 21/45/90 for Forge 1/2/3+. Gemcutting eligibility follows Gemcutting level. Manufacturing a stronger item or gem does not let you equip it before meeting its requirements.
