Passive tree

The character tree has three separate point pools. Follow connected paths out from your class start, using Travel points for roads, Passive points for combat bonuses and Mastery points for a chosen bonus in a cluster.

How points are earned

  • Travel: 3 per character level. These pay for Travel and Highway nodes and Cosmic Gateways.
  • Passive: 2 per character level, plus 1 for each act boss you defeat. Small, Large, Notable, Doomed and Keystone nodes use these.
  • Mastery: 1 every 5 character levels, up to 20. A Mastery becomes available after you allocate a Notable in its cluster.

Paths, Masteries and refunds

A node must connect to your allocated tree. Other classes' start nodes are closed paths unless you are the Exile. Each Mastery offers three bonuses; the same named Mastery bonus cannot be used twice anywhere on your tree.

Refunding a node or changing a Mastery choice costs one Orb of Regret. You cannot refund a node if that would cut other allocated nodes off from your start, or leave a Mastery without a Notable in its cluster.

Cosmic Gateways

The six Gateways form three pairs: Solar/Lunar, Astral/Void and Primal/Divine. Allocating one end costs 4 Travel points and makes its paired end count as a new place from which you can continue allocating. You do not need to buy a road across the space between them.

Doomed Gates

Each Doomed Gate costs 5 Passive points and opens the powerful wheel behind it. The price is permanent while that Gate is allocated:

  • Blood Toll: 25% less maximum Life.
  • Brittle Bones: take 20% increased Physical Damage.
  • Glass Veil: Armour is set to 0.
  • Withered Veins: flasks recover no Life or Mana.
  • Shattered Mind: skills cost 100% more Mana.
  • Broken Covenant: -30% to all Elemental Resistances.

Profiles

You can keep three character-tree profiles. Profile 1 is open immediately, Profile 2 opens in Act 5, and Profile 3 opens when you reach Tier 5 Maps. Switching first saves the tree you are leaving. The first time you switch to an empty profile, it begins as a copy of your current tree.

Use Profile Settings to rename a profile and link a saved gear set or skill-bar loadout. Those linked loadouts switch with the profile. The Atlas strategy field labels the intended Atlas setup; Atlas nodes are managed on the Map Device.
