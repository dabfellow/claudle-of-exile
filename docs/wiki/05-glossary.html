Glossary

What the game's terms mean. For the exact numbers, see Damage and ailments.

Combat

Ailment
A lasting effect from a hit: burn, ignite, chill, freeze, shock, poison or bleed.
Armour
Reduces physical hits. It works best against small hits.
Buildup
Fire, cold and lightning ailments fill a bar on the target. Filling it causes Ignite or Freeze, or a full-strength Shock.
Channel
A long boss spell that pulses as it grows. A stun, freeze or interrupt breaks it.
Curse
A lasting weakness on an enemy. An enemy carries one at a time, and bosses take half the effect.
Energy shield
A layer of protection over your life that recharges when you stop being hit.
Evasion Rating
Gives a chance to dodge hits. It can't dodge a boss's telegraphed moves.
Guard
A defensive skill, such as a block or a shield.
Heavy
A skill that winds up first and lands at the end.
Increased and more
All your "increased" bonuses add together. Each "more" bonus multiplies on its own, so it's usually stronger.
Mega Crit
A flat 400% hit that ignores your crit multiplier.
Resistance
Cuts fire, cold or lightning damage. Yours cap at 75%.
Rings
Distance on the battlefield. Ring 1 is next to you. Melee skills reach ring 1.
Stagger
Stuns on a boss add up, and a full bar staggers it.
Wind-up
A telegraphed boss move, shown before it lands so you can answer it.

Class mechanics

Rage
Warrior. Hits you deal build Rage, for more attack damage and more Armour.
Momentum
Ranger. Attacks build attack speed, and being hit halves it.
Mana is Power
Mage. Spells deal more damage the fuller your mana is.
Riposte
Duelist. Blocking or evading a hit counter-attacks.
Fervor
Templar. Hits you take fill it, and a full bar releases a healing nova.
Malice
Shadow. Non-critical hits fill it, and a full bar makes your next hit a Coup de Grâce.

Items

Base
The kind of item, which sets its starting stats and what can roll on it.
Implicit
A built-in modifier that comes with the base.
Item level
Decides which modifier tiers an item can roll.
Prefix and suffix
The two kinds of rolled modifier.
Tier
How strong a modifier's roll is. Better tiers need a higher item level.
Weight
How likely a modifier is to be picked, compared with the others that can roll.

Idle systems

Rule
A condition that tells a skill when to fire.
War Table
The Hideout station that unlocks reactive rules and more rule slots.
Watchtower
The Hideout station that raises your offline limit.
Offline haul
The pile that holds items found while you're away.
Instill
Makes a flask use itself on a condition.
Trial
A challenge that grants ascendancy points.
