Meals and cooking

Cooking is available without campaign progress. It turns gathered plants into meals and into the special ingredients used for Sacred Life, Sacred Mana and Sulphur flasks. Recipes still require their skill levels and ingredients.

Meal effects

You can have one meal active. Combat meals last {{data:meal-length}} minutes of time spent fighting (pausing in town and during life skills). Limited skilling meals (Gatherer's Ration and Artisan's Lunch) tick only during matching foreground work, while signature dishes (Moonfall Consommé and Starlight Banquet) and 5-hour feast projects (Grand Artisan's Feast and Wayfarer's Feast) provide high-end buffs. A different meal replaces all remaining time on the old one. Cooking 30+ also prepares Binding Stock for pinnacle crafting.

Keep eating

Turn on Keep eating to consume another copy of the same meal from the shared bank when the current one expires. This works during offline combat. If there is no copy left, the meal ends normally.

Cooking tree bonuses

  • Second Helping: 15% chance to cook two items for one set of ingredients.
  • Slow Simmer: meals last 25% longer.
  • Head Chef: meal stats are {{data:meal-head-chef}}% stronger, but Cooking is 25% slower.
