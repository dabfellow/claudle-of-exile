Meals and cooking

Cooking opens in Black Harbor in Act 5. It turns gathered plants into meals and into the special ingredients used for Sacred Life, Sacred Mana and Sulphur flasks.

Meal effects

You can have one meal active. Every meal lasts {{data:meal-length}} minutes of time spent fighting; its clock pauses in town and during life skills. A different meal replaces all remaining time on the old one.

{{data:meals}}

Keep eating

Turn on Keep eating to consume another copy of the same meal from the shared bank when the current one expires. This works during offline combat. If there is no copy left, the meal ends normally.

Cooking tree bonuses

  • Second Helping: 15% chance to cook two items for one set of ingredients.
  • Slow Simmer: meals last 25% longer.
  • Head Chef: meal stats are {{data:meal-head-chef}}% stronger, but Cooking is 25% slower.
