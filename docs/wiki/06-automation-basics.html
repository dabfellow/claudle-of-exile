Automation basics

A skill on your bar only fires by itself if you give it a rule. Gems start on Manual only, so a new skill does nothing until you set one on the Skills page. Your Default Attack needs no rule: it's what you do when nothing else is ready.

Your first rules

  • Main attack: Whenever ready.
  • Area skill: When enemies at least 3, so it isn't wasted on one target.
  • Buff: When its effect is not active.
  • Big cooldown: Only against bosses.
  • Mana-hungry skill: When mana above 40%, so it leaves enough for your guard.

Each gem also has a suggested rule, offered in the rule editor. It falls back to Whenever ready if you haven't unlocked that rule yet.

Unlocking more rules

Simple rules are free. The War Table in your Hideout teaches the reactive ones, for good, across your account. Its levels also add more rule slots per skill.

Several rules on one skill

When a skill has more than one rule, all of them must pass. For example, "Only against bosses" and "When mana above 40%" means it fires only on bosses, and only with the mana to spare.

Which skill goes first

  1. A skill you pressed yourself goes next, ignoring its rule.
  2. Then skills are tried by priority: High, Normal, then Low.
  3. Within a priority, skills with a cooldown go before cooldown-free attacks.
  4. After that, bar order breaks the tie, left to right.
  5. If nothing can fire, you use your Default Attack.

This is why a cooldown-free attack doesn't crowd out your guard. If two cooldown skills compete, raise the one that matters to High.

Examples

  • Healing: a heal on When life below 50%.
  • Boss wind-ups: a guard on When an enemy winds up.
  • Breaking a channel: {{skill:war_cry}} on When an enemy channels. A wind-up rule doesn't fire for a channel.
  • Ailments: a skill that needs a bleed on While target is affected by Bleed.
  • Bad ground: a movement skill on While standing on, to step out at once.
