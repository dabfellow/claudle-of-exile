Boss preparation

Bosses hit harder than anything around them, and their big moves are telegraphed. Here's how to get ready. This is advice, and the mechanics list below comes from the game's own data.

Before the fight

  • Read the boss's page for its damage type and mechanics.
  • Check your resistances against its element. Yours cap at 75%.
  • Refill your flasks, and know which key is which.
  • Pick up your loot. A death marks what's on the ground.
  • Set your rules. A guard on When an enemy winds up answers most big moves.
  • Check you're at the right level. Bosses are usually a little above their area.

If you keep dying

  • Upgrade the gear with your lowest resistance or life first.
  • Spend your passive points.
  • Add a heal or a second guard, and set it to High priority.
  • Drop back an area and level up. A lost boss fight sends it back into hiding, so you'll need to reach its kill count again.

Leaving a boss to your rules

Offline, a boss only comes out once your rules could finish it. See Bosses while you're away.
