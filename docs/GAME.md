# Game reference

Last checked 2026-11-06 against the repository. Released baseline: 0.4.2. Local changes are called out below; they are not automatically live. Exact item, recipe, node and monster numbers belong in `src/data`, not duplicated tables here. Run `npm run wiki` for the player reference generated from those tables. Future designs belong only in [MILESTONES.md](MILESTONES.md).

## Character and progression

Path of Idle combines automatic ARPG combat, equipment and passive builds with gathering and production. Combat and skilling use one activity slot; greenhouse growth, Expedition orders and other passive updates can continue alongside it. Manual play improves control without replacing the idle loop.

Six main classes have different starts, bonuses, starter skills and base mechanics. Their mechanics exist before ascendancy; the specialised mechanic nodes moved out of the character tree into ascendancies.

| Class | Base mechanic | Ascendancies |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Rage from hits: more attack damage and Armour; decays between packs | Ravager, Anvil |
| Duelist | Riposte counters on block/evade and weaker counters on landed hits | Matador, Vanguard |
| Ranger | Momentum: attack speed and Evasion; flat stack losses on landed hits (2, or 4 for a hit of 15%+ Life); each Momentum-keeping source saves 1 stack, minimum loss 1 | Longshot, Falconer |
| Shadow | Malice from non-crits; full meter enables a Coup de Grâce | Headsman, Plaguewright |
| Mage | Mana is Power rewards Mana spent in the previous four seconds | Wellspring, Alchemist |
| Templar | Fervor from incoming hits; full meter releases a healing holy nova | Flagellant, Lightkeeper |

The Exile unlocks by completing the campaign. It has no class mechanic, class bonus or ascendancy, and can select the centre or another class's start. See `src/data/classes.ts` and `src/engine/mechanics.ts` for current bonuses and exceptions.

Character XP, life-skill XP and gem XP are separate. Area level ranges modify character and gem XP, discouraging unlimited levelling in early areas. Ordinary deaths remove XP equal to 5% of the current-to-next-level requirement, capped at progress already earned so no level is lost. Death ends combat and marks ground loot. A later death removes marked items still unclaimed. Retreat refills flasks. Hardcore overrides ordinary death recovery.

**Local combat XP bands:** No adjustment inside the area's level range or within two levels of either edge. When enemies are above the player (below the range), the bonus is 5% at three levels, 12.5% at four and capped at 20% from five onward. When enemies are below the player (above the range), the penalty follows a quadratic curve rounded to whole percentages: 10%, 11%, 15%, 21%, 30%, 41%, 54%, 70% at three through ten levels respectively, capped at 70% thereafter. Each further level adds a larger penalty until the cap. Character and gem kill XP share this multiplier, including offline; labels and the wiki XP calculator use the same bands. Source: `src/engine/character.ts`.

## Campaign and Maps

All six acts are open. The campaign graph includes prerequisite bosses, forks and side areas; it is not one linear list. Sources: `src/data/zones.ts`, `src/data/continent.ts`, `src/engine/progress.ts`.

| Act | Region | Distinctive mechanic | New life skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Drowned Coast | Slams, novas, summons, enrage and wards | Mining, Woodcutting, Foraging, Smithing, Alchemy, Gemcutting |
| 2 | The Burning Steppe | Scorched ground | Thieving and Heist |
| 3 | The City of Saints | Martyrs protect chained bosses; area/chain damage or suitable aim reaches them | Rune-carving |
| 4 | The Hollow Peaks | Frostbite and frozen ground; guards, movement and flasks provide answers | Archaeology and Expedition |
| 5 | The Sunless Sea | Channels that need interruption or a stun where applicable | Cooking |
| 6 | The Shattered Throne | Echoes and unholy ground | Cartography |

Maps are playable after campaign completion; dev/admin preview access is an additional shortcut. **Local Maps redesign:** Ten plain tiers (`map_t1`–`map_t10`, monster levels 66–84, 6–12 packs) share a realm-wide map supply (`s.account.maps`, synced with `s.maps.stock`, migrating legacy themed maps by tier). Each run borrows a random campaign area's visuals, monster roster and boss mechanics whose required automation rules the player has unlocked. A map is spent on entry, including on death or retreat (unless refunded by the Unbroken Path Atlas keystone on death). Clear its packs and boss to finish; auto-run repeats the selected tier until its stock ends or the activity stops. Each new run restores Life, Mana and ES. Fleeing an entire field returns a pack rather than crediting it as cleared.

Level 60+ campaign monsters can drop T1 maps once Act 6 is open (with a 50% first-kill chance on level 60+ campaign bosses), and defeating the Last King tops up the realm's map supply to at least five maps. Tier groups carry permanent danger and reward identities: T1–2 baseline, T3–4 two Elite packs (4× Life, 1.5× damage, 3× XP, +60% drops; Chaos emphasis), T5–6 Twin boss lieutenant at 60% stats sharing a Life pool (first map unique pool and fourth ascendancy trial at T5), T7–8 boss ground hazard (second unique pool and Regal emphasis), T9 permanent monster enrage (+30% speed, +40% damage; Exalted emphasis), and T10 Pinnacle combining Elite packs, Twin boss and permanent enrage (T10 unique pool and Divine emphasis). Apprentice (T1+), Journeyman (T5+) and Master (T8+) Sextants roll six-charge Danger/Reward/Both/Hunting modifiers across up to four Astrolabe slots (1–3 from Map Device levels 1–4 plus 1 from Surveying). The realm-shared Atlas Tree (`s.account.atlas`) is a connected 100-node radial skill tree across Sustain, Bounty, Surveying and Peril wings. Players can earn and allocate up to 70 points: 30 across T1–T10 (first clear, all Sextant slots filled, and deathless clear per tier), 2 for each Doomed Map tier's first clear, 3 for each Hard Boss first clear, and 5 for each Uber Boss first clear. These rewards are recorded once across the realm. The tree includes the Unbroken Path and Doomed Horizon (20-point) keystones. With Doomed Horizon allocated, T8–T10 bosses can drop Doomed Maps I–V (levels 86–94, 14–16 packs, 2–4 rolled Doomed modifiers, 3.5× T10 Exalted/Divine rates, Fractured Orbs, and 10/15/20/25/35% Uber Key drops). Cartography draws T1 maps, binds T1 folios, upgrades T2–T10 maps (3→1, or 2→1 with Master Cartographer), and crafts all three Sextants. Sources: `src/data/maps.ts`, `src/data/cartography.ts`, `src/engine/maps.ts`, `src/ui/pages/MapDevicePage.tsx`.

## Combat, skills and automation

The battlefield is a hex half-ring with the player at its bottom, incoming packs, walking melee monsters and ranged enemies. Range, shapes, chains, pierce, obstructions and closing-in affect target access. Click targeting and priority/order settings influence aim. Skill-specific aim requires the Aim unlock. Sources: `src/engine/battlefield.ts`, `src/engine/combat.ts`.

The six-slot skill bar holds active gems, each with its own supports, rules and priority. Gems begin Manual only; the editor offers a suggested rule. All conditions on a skill must pass. A queued manual skill goes first, then High/Normal/Low priority; within a priority, cooldown/area/stacking skills precede ordinary cooldown-free attacks, then slot order breaks ties. If none can fire, Default Attack runs. Action time and cooldown are separate. Trigger supports can override ordinary selection.

Vitals, Wind-up and Ground rules and two condition slots are baseline. War Table 2 grants Status, 3 grants Aim, 4 grants a third condition, and 5/6/7 unlock Normal/Magic/Rare auto-salvage. Unlocks are shared and only increase. Rules do not ignore weapon, attribute, level, range, resource or cooldown requirements. Automated Life costs preserve the configured Life reserve. Lower-priority skills preserve Mana for eligible higher-priority skills becoming ready within 1.5 seconds. Sources: `src/engine/automation.ts`, `src/data/hideout.ts`, `src/engine/gems.ts`.

Brawler, Kiter and Cautious postures control spacing and movement. Kiter pushes nearby non-boss enemies back periodically. Cautious uses a slower pace and an emergency flee condition tied to depleted Life flasks. The separate emergency-flee option remains. Legacy movement settings still exist for old saves and low-level callers.

**Flask status:** 0.4.2 automatically used unenchanted recovery flasks at low resources. The pending local change removes that default: manual use or an Instilling Orb enchantment is required. Charges refill on kills; utility effects and recovery have their own charge and duration rules. Enchantment conditions are defined in `src/data/flasks.ts`; crafting lives in `src/engine/flasks.ts`.

Skill gems reach level 20. Standard attribute requirements scale from 8 to 155, with demanding gems reaching 178. Requirements still make off-attribute gems an investment. Supports fit by tags/effects or an explicit skill restriction. The Gem shop stocks reachable gems; monsters do not drop skill/support gems. Gemcutting is another source. Sources: `src/data/gems.ts`, `src/engine/gems.ts`, `src/engine/hideout.ts`.

## Damage, defence and summons

Attacks use weapon damage and skill scaling; dual wielding averages weapons and adds attack speed. Spells use their own base damage, spell/type increases, flat spell damage and appropriate supports. Generic flat elemental damage has reduced effectiveness for spells. Increased damage adds within its category; more multipliers multiply. Accuracy, criticals, conversion, resistance, guard effects and conditional mechanics are applied by the engine. Gear comparison computes skill and defence changes, not a universal item score. The combat DPS meter measures damage dealt; the equipment estimate is not that meter.

Physical, fire, cold, lightning and chaos are supported damage types, including player chaos resistance. Burn builds toward Ignite, Chill toward Freeze, and Shock scales with buildup. Poison stacks; Bleed keeps its strongest effect. Ailment chances, buildup, duration and damage are not interchangeable stats. Armour reduces physical hits; Evasion avoids eligible hits; ES absorbs damage and recharges after a delay. Heavy boss wind-ups have special avoidance/guard rules. The generated wiki's Damage page reads formula constants from code; use `src/engine/gems.ts`, `src/engine/stats.ts` and `src/engine/combat.ts` when auditing exact calculations.

Minions occupy cells, move, attack, take hits and can block routes. Their damage uses player skill calculations and applicable minion modifiers. Summoning rules can replenish the crew. Persistent minions can remain between packs; timed summons expire. Storms are persistent skill areas with repeated pulses, duration and per-skill limits. Sources: `src/engine/battlefield.ts`, `src/engine/combat.ts`, `src/engine/gems.ts`.

**Local monster identities:** Campaign and map monsters (including bosses and ordinary summons) receive creature-based archetypes: heavy brutes, ice golems, armoured guardians, evasive fighters, frail swarmers, venom creatures, shielded spirits, casters and raking beasts. Definitions can explicitly override the creature classifier. They use player Armour, accuracy/Evasion, resistance caps, attack/spell block, critical and ES absorption rules. Chaos and poison bypass ES. Regular monsters recharge 5% of maximum ES per second after one second without damage; bosses retain their authored ward recovery instead of gaining free regenerating shields. Poison stacks, Bleed keeps the strongest effect, fire pools burns toward Ignite, cold builds Chill/Freeze, and lightning builds Shock. Combat cards and the generated monster reference expose these defences and attack intervals. Existing saved fights retain their original stats; newly spawned enemies use profiles.

Profiles redistribute the existing Life pool into Life/ES and compensate for Armour, Evasion and block against a fixed reference (half attacks/half spells, half physical/one eighth each other damage type, level-scaled accuracy and hit size). The same reference effective pool and expected raw basic DPS are retained, with criticals and ailment damage included in the basic damage budget. Slow attacks hit harder; fast attacks hit softer. This is a calibration target, not identical difficulty for every build: damage type, hit size, chaos bypass, recovery and control still create strengths and counters. Pack sizes, loot, XP, boss wind-up damage and rarity multipliers retain their existing budgets. Sources: `src/data/monsterProfiles.ts`, `src/engine/monsters.ts`, `src/engine/combat.ts`, `tests/monsterProfiles.test.ts`.

**Local, unreleased balance changes:** Armour uses `armour / (armour + 2 × hit)` with a 90% cap; Evasion caps at 85%; ES recharges 30%/s after one second. Monster damage ramps above level 20. Magic packs and rare monsters have rarity multipliers and visible modifiers, including regeneration, leech, resistance, evasion penetration and area protection. These edits and the larger slot-specific affix pool are present in the worktree, not the 0.4.2 release. Chaos-hit ES bypass and Maps skill mechanics are also local work. Do not infer release readiness from these descriptions.

## Trees

The character tree builds offence, defence and recovery across six connected regions around Exile's Crossing. Class starts create affinities rather than exclusive lanes. Regional overlaps support blocking/Bleed, fast accurate attacks, projectile ailments, crit/chaos spells, elemental ES and elemental shield attacks.

Its point budget is character level plus one (two points at level 1). Nodes require connectivity from the selected start; the tree's ordinary paths are not gated by act milestones.

Keep clusters compact: lines, forks and wheels should put a meaningful destination within two or three allocated points. Attribute roads retain +10 pure or +5/+5 hybrid attributes because gems and gear need them. Avoid reinstating class-mechanic beelines. Balance complete routes at 5/10/20/30 points, including attributes, defence, travel and useful alternatives. Do not substitute more travel for a balanced notable. Masteries offer a chosen thematic effect; keystones change a rule with a cost. Connectivity rules prevent orphaned refunds. Sources: `src/data/passiveTree.ts`, `src/engine/passives.ts`.

Each of the twelve ascendancies has six notable branches (three signature and three class-mechanic) and two capstone branches. A notable path costs two points; a capstone path costs three. The gate is free, and all branches are available after choosing the ascendancy. Trials grant 2/2/3/2 points at Pilgrim Gate, Giants' Throne, Exiles' Gate and the first T5 map boss. Nine points allow any three notables and either capstone. There is no hard node-count limit: future item-granted nodes may exceed that combination. Such chase items are planned, not implemented by the ordinary allocation budget. Refunds and switching have material/Regret costs. Sources: `src/data/ascendancies.ts`, `src/engine/ascendancy.ts`.

The skilling tree is separate and represents specialisation rather than eventual completion. It grants one point per ten total levels across the eleven implemented noncombat skills, excluding Fishing, to a maximum of 36 points. Branch requirements can include a skill level. Regret refunds must preserve connectivity. Sources: `src/data/skillTree.ts`, `src/engine/skillTree.ts`.

## Gathering, production and side systems

Higher actions require the stated skill level, area and sometimes station upgrade. Costs, times, yields and XP come from `src/data/skills.ts`. Production stops when inputs or capacity run out. Smithing item level is capped by campaign progress. Active minigames grant temporary Focus speed up to 45%; offline skilling keeps its ordinary rate and does not earn active Focus. Overtime is a separate offline tree bonus.

Every eligible timed non-combat action and Heist job also builds character-owned mastery in one of 67 stable, authored work bands. Completed work grants its unmodified base duration as practice whether performed live, offline, by auto-repeat or through the Greenhouse; interrupted work grants none. Practice reaches level 100 at 36,000 nominal seconds. Its continuous bonus reaches at most 12 percentage points of increased speed, plus either 8% expected ordinary primary yield for gathering or 8% expected conservation of explicitly eligible ordinary production inputs. Heists receive speed only. Rare and chase rewards, keys, admission costs, maps, item generation and access requirements are never improved or bypassed. Mastery progress and bonuses appear on action and Heist cards, and offline gains are summarized. Sources: `src/data/skillingMastery.ts`, `src/engine/skillingMastery.ts`, `src/engine/skills.ts`, `src/engine/heist.ts`.

| System | Current loop | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Mining, Woodcutting, Foraging | Gather ore/logs/herbs; bonus fossils, resins, essences and shards support crafts | `src/data/skills.ts`, `src/data/craftMats.ts` |
| Smithing | Make item bases; refine ore/logs into bars/planks for station upgrades | `src/data/skills.ts`, `src/engine/skills.ts` |
| Alchemy | Brew flasks and craft configured currencies | `src/data/skills.ts` |
| Gemcutting | Produce active/support gems from reachable pools | `src/engine/skills.ts`, `src/engine/gems.ts` |
| Thieving / Heist | Lift gold/Markers, choose exit depth, run timed rooms; alarms reduce take; recase when Markers run out; train and select crew | `src/data/heist.ts`, `src/engine/heist.ts` |
| Rune-carving | Make six rune families in four grades; one rune slot per item, slot-specific effects and same-family resonance | `src/data/runes.ts`, `src/engine/runes.ts` |
| Archaeology / Expedition | Dig six sites for faction Artifacts, permanent strata/finds and standing; spend at restocking merchants or use standing orders | `src/data/expedition.ts`, `src/engine/expedition.ts` |
| Cooking | Make meals and ingredients for Sacred/Sulphur flasks; one meal active, timed during combat, optional automatic re-eating | `src/data/cooking.ts`, `src/engine/cooking.ts` |
| Cartography | Make vellum/inks, draw T1 maps, bind folios, upgrade maps through T10, and craft Apprentice/Journeyman/Master Sextants | `src/data/cartography.ts`, `src/engine/cartography.ts` |
| Greenhouse | Passive herb production alongside the main activity, including offline | `src/engine/skills.ts`, `src/engine/state.ts` |

Runes sit outside ordinary affixes: orbs do not reroll them; Wax impressions do not restore them; replacing one destroys the old rune. They work on uniques. Uninscribed runes and crafting materials are in the shared bank. Forger's Seals protect a selected modifier during supported crafts; Locksmith's Wax records/restores an item's ordinary crafted state. Ancient Whetstones raise one eligible modifier tier, limited by item level and the one-honed-mod rule. Source implementations define exclusions.

Fishing is a permanent joke/placeholder, not a promised future skill. Fletching and other unimplemented skill ideas remain roadmap material.

## Gear, crafting, storage and hideout

Normal, Magic, Rare and Unique gear use bases with level/attribute/slot requirements, implicits and, where applicable, random modifiers. Magic supports one prefix and suffix; Rare up to three each. Item level gates affix tiers; groups prevent incompatible duplicates. Local weapon/defence modifiers affect the item itself. Bases extend to level 80. Unique definitions and drop pools generate [UNIQUES.md](UNIQUES.md); do not hand-edit it.

Orbs, fossils, essences, resins, Seals, Wax, Whetstones, rune inscription and one bench-crafted modifier provide distinct crafting tools. The built Crafting Bench enables salvage; its level 5 enables repeat-until auto-crafting as an activity. Field Tinkering substitutes 3/4/5 Arcane Dust for a missing Transmutation/Augmentation/Alteration on item-level-45-or-lower gear, with the bench built. Source: `src/engine/crafting.ts`, `src/engine/craftUntil.ts`.

Bag capacity is 60; ground pile 50; offline haul 200; stash pages 80 each, with three starting pages and purchasable expansion. Ground/haul overflow discards oldest items. A dedicated unlimited Uniques tab also records discoveries. Bulk rarity salvage skips runed items; manual item actions handle individual choices. Stash sort controls exist. Source: `src/engine/storage.ts`, `src/engine/itemSort.ts`, `src/engine/itemActions.ts`.

The realm account shares hideout levels, automation, stash tabs, materials/currency, spare gems/supports/flasks and the Exile unlock. Worn gear, bag/ground/haul, level, passives, loadouts and activity state are character-owned. Saved gear sets reference owned item UIDs; skill sets record gems, supports, rules and priority. Automatic gear switching requires Crafting Bench 3 and occurs when an activity changes; missing/unusable pieces can leave a partial set. Source: `src/engine/account.ts`, `src/engine/loadouts.ts`.

Hideout stations are Stash, Forge, Alchemy Table, Crafting Bench, Town Market, Map Device, War Table and Watchtower. Upgrades use Gold, refined planks/bars, Rough Gems and Boss Trophies. First campaign boss kills guarantee a trophy; repeat eligible boss kills have a 25% chance. The top upgrades of the Map Device, Forge, Alchemy Table, Crafting Bench, Watchtower and Trophy Altar also take Hard Boss trophies (Marrow Shards, Choir Glass, Black Pearl, Throne Ash, Crownless Sigil), which also pay for high-tier Bench crafts at Bench levels 6–7. Costs and exact unlocks are authoritative in `src/data/hideout.ts`. Town's NPC shops and Gem shop are distinct from the Player Market. Paid NPC restock grows more expensive until the free restock.

## Accounts, modes, market and offline

The title offers an unsaved demo and email/password, Google or Discord sign-in when Supabase is configured. Existing local guest characters can be carried into an account. Character saves and shared realm pools have local storage/cloud paths. Export/import, versioned migrations and reset controls exist. Cloud merging is not simply a promise that the newest timestamp always wins; inspect `src/platform/saves.ts`, `src/platform/accounts.ts`, `src/game.ts` and account transfer logic before changing persistence.

Hardcore, Solo Self-Found and Classic are creation-time toggles that can combine. Hardcore death is permanent. SSF creates a character-isolated realm and blocks the Player Market. Classic blocks noncombat activities and substitutes Gold for gathered/component/trophy upgrade costs; appropriate skilling-gated bench recipes are exempted. Hardcore/Classic combinations also separate shared realms and trading. Sources: `src/engine/challenge.ts`, `src/engine/character.ts`.

The signed-in Player Market unlocks after Act 1 in the relevant realm. It supports buy-now listings, auctions, bids, cancellation and deliveries. Transactions hold local items/Gold before a server request and reconcile unresolved holds later. Supabase functions validate item legality and settle transactions atomically; expiring auctions settle on subsequent requests, not a server timer. Pending wallet deliveries must be collected. Admins can remove listings/ban sellers and upload the current item validation rules after content changes. The client owns the game simulation, so this is not server-authoritative anti-cheat. Sources: `src/engine/market.ts`, `src/platform/market.ts`, `docs/ops/market.sql`.

**Local market hardening:** Invalid/non-integer payments, unsupported currencies and duplicate pending operation ids are refused before taking local goods. Updated SQL denies direct table/sequence access, pins privileged function lookup paths, rejects null payment fields and excessive searches, and limits successful list/buy/bid operations to 60 per account per minute across realms. The SQL must be applied separately to Supabase. Offline-earned goods remain tradable by owner decision: these checks cannot prove acquisition, balances or progression, because cloud saves and the simulation remain client-owned. Legal-looking forged goods and save rollback remain residual cheating risks; a client-generated signature or a server wallet funded by unverified client deposits would not solve them.

Offline simulation uses the same engine, capped at eight hours initially and up to 24 via Watchtower. Manual queued actions are discarded. A death stops fighting; greenhouse/other applicable passive work can continue. New bosses may be fought offline with the baseline Wind-up unlock, but martyr, Frostbite and channel bosses also require their specific automatic answers; a boss can wait while trash farming continues. Resource exhaustion and activity limits stop jobs. Offline loot goes to the haul. Source: `src/engine/offline.ts`, `src/engine/automation.ts`.

Achievements award configured currency, unlocks or uniques; The Unbroken Temper is an achievement reward rather than a normal drop. The Old Covenant (no-trade leech ring) is not a drop either: characters created before the skill re-sort moved early leech receive it once as a make-up gift (`src/engine/account.ts`, save migration 32). Music has per-device mute/volume and pauses while hidden. Settings support reduced motion, feedback/reviews, save tools and salvage preferences. Admin tools expose usage, feedback, reviews, events, market moderation and Sentry links. Scheduled/manual events apply the strongest active bonus of each kind instead of multiplying overlapping copies. Sources: `src/data/achievements.ts`, `src/data/events.ts`, `src/platform`, `src/ui/pages/AdminPage.tsx`.

**Local Admin overhaul (requires `docs/ops/admin.sql`):** Overview leads with Created → Acts 1–6 → Maps milestones for current cloud characters, excludes account-storage rows, normalizes class casing and shows empty level buckets. Death-zone diagnostics include retained deleted-character backups; historical death locations cannot be reconstructed. Anonymous demo-start/Act-1-boss/conversion counters are deduplicated per random tab session, respect Do Not Track and show the last 30 days; they are approximate client-reported counts, not unique visitors or a matched retention cohort.

Players can be searched by name, email or id. The inspector exposes last cloud save, area, level/class, simulated playtime, game/account JSON and restore points. The database retains ten pre-update character backups, at most one per five minutes plus the state before deletion. Deleted characters remain discoverable while backups exist. Audited restores create a separate recovered character, stop its activity/maps and leave the current character and shared stash intact; they do not roll back shared resources. Backups start when the migration is installed. A sandbox copies state/account into a fresh identity and memory-only save store, disables trading and restores the admin's original character on exit; reproductions never upload to the target or admin's normal saves.

New feedback includes character identity, equipment, skill priority/supports/rules, passives/masteries/ascendancy, posture, bounded combat state and ten recent log lines. Snapshots omit stash/ground/bag contents and auth/session data, use a 192 KiB client budget and a 256 KiB database limit, and identify partial captures. Existing reports have no retroactive snapshots. Account email is joined on the server for admins; reported character ids are verified against the submitting account before offering a direct inspector link. Older database context limits fall back to sending the compact report rather than losing feedback. Type/version filters and numeric-version bulk resolution are server-backed. Replies and optional currency/item compensation are admin-only, audited and idempotent per request; signed-in players read mail in Settings and collect compensation in its realm, including before Act 1 or in SSF. Gifts use the market's item/currency rules and existing delivery/recovery model; they do not make the client-owned economy authoritative.

Custom events and broadcasts support all/combat+gem/life-skill XP, life-skill speed, currency-orb drops, loot rolls, vendor restock speed and item-crafting material conservation. Fractional loot rolls are sampled, not rounded up. Cost reductions conserve whole materials probabilistically while requiring the normal base cost to start; Orbs, Essences, Wax, Whetstones and bench crafts are covered, including auto-crafting. Events show Live/Scheduled/Inactive, countdowns and Stop controls; bonuses use the strongest applicable value. Games refresh changes within five minutes. Offline catch-up splits at recorded schedule boundaries; only the currently retained window of a reused event is available, and legacy manual rows with no start timestamp cannot supply historical activation times. Sources: `src/platform/admin.ts`, `src/ui/components/Admin*`, `src/data/events.ts`, `src/engine/offline.ts`, `docs/ops/admin.sql`.

Overview diagnostics refresh every 30 seconds while open; the inspector has an explicit latest-save refresh. Developer mail is checked on sign-in and every minute, with an unread notice linking to Settings. Admin compensation may replace no-trade Unique rewards through a separate admin-only catalogue; ordinary market listings still reject those items.

After the Admin migration, cloud row timestamps are stamped on database receipt so a player's incorrect clock cannot forge the inspector's latest-save time. Existing stored timestamps are not retroactively corrected.
